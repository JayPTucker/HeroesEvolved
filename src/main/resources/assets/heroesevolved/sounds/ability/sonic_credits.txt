Sonic Resonance: original synthesized sound design

sonic_cry.ogg, sonic_burst.ogg and sonic_scream.ogg are generated from scratch by
tools/generate-sonic-audio.py. No recordings, samples or third-party audio are used.

Each sound layers a formant-shaped, chorused harmonic shriek (Black Canary's cry), a
bowed cello-like drone (Emma Coolidge's cello), a band-limited pressure blast, and
glassy high partials, placed in a synthetic decaying-noise room. 48 kHz mono Vorbis.

The previous reference-cry excerpts have been removed from the mod.
