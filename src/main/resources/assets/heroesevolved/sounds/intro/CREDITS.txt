Human panic recordings
======================
"Crowd in panic" by IENBA (2019)
https://freesound.org/people/IENBA/sounds/491405/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Recorded and performed by Video Games and Animation students at the National
School of Arts of Uruguay during a Sound Design Workshop (Licenciatura en
Lenguajes y Medios Audiovisuales), as credited on the source page.

Input: publicly available HQ MP3 rendition of the CC0 recording:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/491/491405_9616576-hq.mp3

Edits for Heroes Evolved: three short excerpts at 2.4-5.4, 12.5-15.5 and
24.8-28.0 seconds; mono downmix, 44.1 kHz resampling, removal of sub-bass,
gentle high-frequency rolloff, level normalization, and fades. No synthesized
voices or changed vocal identities. Output files: crowd_panic.ogg,
crowd_panic_2.ogg and crowd_panic_3.ogg.

Original procedural assets
==========================
nuclear_siren.ogg and nuclear_blast.ogg were synthesized for this project.
The blast combines a pressure crack, bass impulse, turbulent roar and echoes.
Rebuild these assets with tools/generate-intro-audio.py.
