# Reference images

The user's picks for the quality bar (added 2026-11-13). Look at the ones that match the job before
drafting, and again when judging your in-game screenshots against them. `inspiration.md` spells out
what each does well as block recipes; the section numbers below point there.

| `city-block-house.png` | A small city block as one stacked build | mixed materials per volume, awnings, outside stairs, rooftop clutter | 8 |
| `city-block-house-annotated.png` | The same kind of build, labelled | the list of props that make a block feel lived in (bins, dish, wires, sandbox) | 8 |
| `medieval-canal-town.png` | Row houses over a canal | every house different, jettied upper floors, window boxes, a quay wall with weathered stone | 9 |
| `modern-city-plaza.webp` | Downtown towers round a plaza | setbacks and vertical bands on towers, a round fountain plaza, tree-lined streets, crosswalks | 10 |

layout and mood, not its exact proportions or block shapes.
