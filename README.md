# Heroes Evolved

A Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge mod with unique player powers, shared-world story
events and evolving abilities. Build and run with Java 21.

Requires [Simple Voice Chat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simple-voice-chat) and
[Simple Voice Radio](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simple-voice-radio) (with Lexiconfig) on the client
and the server: villages call for help over the radio. See [The emergency band](docs/emergency-band.md).

## Skills and progression

Open the survival inventory and click **Skills**, next to the recipe book.
Every power has three named branches of six nodes: eighteen per power, 342 in all.
The Abilities page is also the skill tree: each branch is drawn as its real tree, and you learn skills and put them on keys right there.
Powers grow from Level 1 to 20 (Manifesting, Controlled, Practiced, Mastered, and Evolved after a
per-power trial). Levels unlock the core kit and give skill points; story notes hidden in the world
give more. Put any ability on any of eight keys, or cast anything from the **ability wheel** (hold R).
Abilities rank up with use. Purchases and loadouts persist across death and relogging. See
[Power Levels, keys and the ability wheel](docs/progression.md).

All eighteen powers have trees: Quake, Flight, Cryokinesis, Electrokinesis,
Telekinesis, Telepathy, Regeneration, Time Manipulation, Radiation, Sonic
Resonance, Superspeed, Pyrokinesis, Invisibility, Density (Phasing) and
Empathic Mimicry, Technopathy, Matter Assembly and [Vibe](docs/vibe.md). Sonic Resonance's cries are original synthesized sound design, including a
positional sustained scream that stops when you release its key.

Three powers also have a **signature ability**, a showpiece hung under one branch's Mastery at Level 10:
[Blackout](docs/electrokinesis.md#signature-blackout) (Electrokinesis drinks every machine around her and
kills the grid), [Singularity](docs/space-time-manipulation.md#signature-singularity) (a black hole with an
accretion disk that drags enemies into orbit, swallows shots and collapses in a shockwave) and
[Phoenix](docs/pyrokinesis.md#signature-phoenix) (a firebird that dives through three enemies and comes home
to heal you).

Electric powers reach real machines from other mods. EMPs drain and stop anything storing Forge Energy
(Mekanism, Thermal, Powah, ...) and seize Create's shafts; lightning charges FE machines; Technopathy enrols
and shuts them down. No machine mod is required. See [Powers and machines](docs/machines.md).

See [Technopathy](docs/technopathy.md) for physical relay networks, remote mechanisms,
prepared traps, stolen projectile trajectories and Machine City. See
[Matter Assembly](docs/matter-assembly.md) for paid temporary walls, bridges, cages,
ricocheting Shards, rescue floors and Cathedral Engine. Both have five core actions,
six optional techniques, a first-awakening sequence and their own Evolved trial.

See [the skill guide](docs/skill-trees.md) for the original optional skills,
point costs, prerequisites and loadout controls.

See [Sylar](docs/sylar.md) for the watchmaker: a slow story told one beat at a time, and a boss
fight where every power he has taken is a real, telegraphed move. Three phases, a boss bar, and he
may run when he is losing.

See [Elle Bishop](docs/elle-bishop.md) for the Company's boss: the day before she comes, lights flicker, redstone
misfires and machines switch themselves on and off around you. Then she fights you on Primatech's test floor,
where every capacitor bank you short out thins her shield, and pulling one she is drinking from backfires into her.

See [Superspeed](docs/superspeed.md) for the reworked speedster, inspired by Daphne Millbrook and the Flash. Momentum crackles,
buffets and breaks the sound barrier. Blitz chains from enemy to enemy, Snatch takes everything in a
blink, Cyclone runs rings, and Lightspeed streaks through everything on the line. At 80 Momentum you
vibrate through walls.

See [Density](docs/density.md) for D.L. Hawkins, reworked from Phasing: a density dial from Full Ghost
to Full Dense, a hand that reaches inside bodies and chests, enemies left stuck in the floor, and
travel underground on a single breath.

See [Quake](docs/quake.md) for Daisy Johnson: Strain makes every quake hit harder until the bones in
her arms crack. Resonate shakes one enemy apart from the inside, Fault Line tears a crack across the
ground under every enemy on it, and crouching still lets her feel heartbeats through the walls.

See [Carrying](docs/carrying.md): sneak + use to pick up anything about your size. People are carried
in your arms, a bridal carry.

See [A Future Unwritten](docs/a-future-unwritten.md) for the expansion that introduced
prophetic paintings and shared reactions between powers.

See [Sonic Resonance](docs/sonic-resonance.md) for the power of Emma Coolidge and Black Canary.
Sound Sight shows every sound around you as colour, through walls. A Resonant Note lures enemies in
and bursts on them, a Wall of Sound turns shots back, and Crescendo builds into one wave.

See [Flight](docs/flight.md) for the rebuilt aerial kit and all eighteen purchasable skills: Momentum, Skyburst, Meteor Drop, Skyhook, Tempest, plus the Sky Guard, Jetstream and Stormbreaker builds.

See [Cryokinesis](docs/cryokinesis.md) for held Rime Stream, solid sculpted walls and ramps,
Glacier Ride, breakable Crystal Prisons and Whiteout, with Sculptor, Glacier and Winterbinder progression.
All six active sub-abilities now have physical projectiles, finite guards, travelling fronts, guided
paths, pressure-bearing rescue shells or staged crystal networks.

See [Regeneration](docs/regeneration.md) for Claire Bennet's reworked kit: she can't be killed (she goes down and gets back up), limbs grow back from the bone out, and Regrowth, Blood Bond and Second Life.

See [Ted Sprague](docs/ted-sprague.md) for the reworked Radiation kit: a Core that makes you stronger as it runs hot, a spreading Radiation Sickness, and a held heat beam.

See [Electrokinesis](docs/electrokinesis.md) for the rebuilt lightning kit: Charge, tap-or-hold Chain Lightning, and the delayed Thunderstrike.

See [Telepathy](docs/telepathy.md) for Mind Threads, command suggestions, shared sight, real-body
Puppet possession with an arrow-key resistance struggle, and private Dreamweaver illusions.

See [Telekinesis](docs/telekinesis.md) for the rebuilt physical force kit and all eighteen skills: orbiting fragments, staggered volleys, Iron Rain, precise grips, projectile catches and aimed force fields. [Power workshop](docs/power-workshop.md) records the original redesign proposals and remaining Mimicry directions.

See [Pyrokinesis and Helixite](docs/pyrokinesis.md) for the fire power, its real-fire rules,
reactions with Cryokinesis, Flight and Superspeed, and the new Helixite crystal ore.
New players wake sealed in a Cryochamber in a stasis bunker beneath world spawn,
centuries after Odessa burned. Search the Overworld for the rare Company relay ruins. The bunker
receiver can give you coordinates, recorded in the Journal (**J**). Restore life support and escort
Mara and Ivo out of the annex while Gabriel Gray approaches. The survivors have an account to
share back at the bunker. See [The Last Signal](docs/the-past.md).

Space-Time Manipulation's [first awakening](docs/time-manipulation-intro.md) takes you and nearby
companions to Odessa at 6:14 to witness the explosion and watch the city run backwards.

See [Invisibility](docs/invisibility.md) for Exposure, optical doubles, shared concealment and
its eighteen redesigned skills. [Phasing](docs/new-powers.md) and [Empathic Mimicry](docs/empathic-mimicry.md) record the
other added powers and the techniques introduced to the original trees.

See [The Formula](docs/formula.md) for awakening unclaimed powers without waiting for an
Eclipse: draw Regeneration blood (donated or forced), spin it in a Centrifuge, synthesize
it in a Synthesis Station built from Helixite, then inject it.

See [Carrying](docs/carrying.md) for carrying mobs and players, safe placement, and the shared movement/visibility rules.

## Building structures

Scenery is drawn as text **blueprints** in `src/main/resources/data/heroesevolved/blueprints/`
and placed by the mod. `tools/blueprint.py` validates, renders and imports them (including
`.nbt` files saved with a vanilla structure block), and `tools/blueprint-preview.init.gradle`
photographs one in-game. Claude Code picks up the workflow and style guide automatically from
the project skill in `.claude/skills/minecraft-building/`.

Operators can open `/heroesevolved admin` to manage players, start the Eclipse, call up the bosses and
set off village events. See [The admin menu](docs/admin-menu.md).

## Development

macOS / Linux (set `JAVA_HOME` to a Java 21 JDK first):

```bash
./gradlew runClient
./gradlew build
./gradlew runGameTestServer --init-script tools/rewind-gametest.init.gradle
python3 tools/verify-skill-content.py
python3 tools/blueprint.py validate src/main/resources/data/heroesevolved/blueprints/*.blueprint
./gradlew runClient --init-script tools/blueprint-preview.init.gradle -Pblueprint=bunker_chamber
```

Windows:

```powershell
.\gradlew.bat runClient
.\gradlew.bat build
.\gradlew.bat runGameTestServer --init-script tools/rewind-gametest.init.gradle
.\tools\verify-effect-geometry.ps1
python tools/verify-skill-content.py
```

Time Stop is a local ability field; it keeps the multiplayer server running.
The test suite is loaded separately and is excluded from the released mod JAR.
