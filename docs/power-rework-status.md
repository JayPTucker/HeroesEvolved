# Power rework status

Audited against the working tree on November 3, 2026. The roster contains **18 powers**;
Sylar is a separate encounter, and so is [Elle Bishop](elle-bishop.md), the Company's boss on Primatech's
test floor (omens the day before, three phases, banks you short out). [Technopathy](technopathy.md) and [Matter Assembly](matter-assembly.md)
are new complete kits, each with five core actions and an eighteen-node interaction tree. [Vibe](vibe.md),
added in November 2026, makes the roster 19: five core actions, an eighteen-node interaction tree and three class abilities.

**Every original core kit has now been redesigned.** Empathic Mimicry was the last: in November 2026 it became
three phases (the power of whoever you are near, sets kept by touch, whole kits remembered), five Echo keys and a
memory wheel ([guide](empathic-mimicry.md)). Cryokinesis now connects Frost,
solid ice construction, Glacier Ride, Crystal Prison and Whiteout. Invisibility is now
a force-field kit: Fade for you and your friends, Lens Shield and Force Push, Force Bridge, Bubble and Crush, and Force Nova. A working power, a bug fix, or an expanded tree
is not by itself a full redesign.

The other fifteen original powers have a substantially redesigned core. That does **not** mean all
their optional skills have received the same treatment. Flight, Telekinesis, Cryokinesis, Invisibility, Induced Radioactivity, Sonic Resonance, Quake, Regeneration, Telepathy and Space-Time Manipulation have complete,
interaction-based eighteen-node redesigns; several other trees still mix
new techniques with older attacks, buffs and generic stat nodes.

## Original kits still pending

None. Empathic Mimicry, the last, was redesigned on November 12, 2026: see [Empathic Mimicry](empathic-mimicry.md).
Its skill tree kept its eighteen nodes (two were reworded for the new kit); its generic stat nodes remain.
