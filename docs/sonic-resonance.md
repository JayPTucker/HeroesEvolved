# Sonic Resonance

Two singers in one power. **Emma Coolidge** lost her hearing and began to *see* sound, in colour, and
her music drew people to it. **Black Canary**'s voice is a weapon. Sonic Resonance is both: you see
every sound around you, you can lure with a note, and you can scream.

Every move has its own arm pose (in third and first person) and its own drawing, in one look: sound as
Emma sees it. Wavefronts ripple outward in rose, amber, mint, azure and violet; waveforms run along every
cry; staves and note heads mark the music. Silence is the same shapes turned around: dull slate rings that
close inward.

## Sound Sight, Resonance and Ringing (always on)

**Sound Sight.** Every sound the game plays near you (within 32 blocks) blooms where it was made, in a
colour that says what made it. Only you see it.

| Colour | Made by |
| --- | --- |
| Red and coral | Monsters |
| Green | Animals |
| Violet | Players |
| Gold | Blocks and footsteps (a footstep is a faint ring on the ground) |
| Blue | The weather and the world |
| Every colour, cycling | Music from a jukebox |

It works through walls, since sound does, so you can follow a zombie around a corner, and an
invisible player's footsteps give them away. Loud sounds bloom larger: a hurt or a break more than a
step, an explosion most of all.

**Resonance** (0 to 100, shown in rainbow under your ability keys):

- **Builds** from sonic hits that land (4 per enemy a Cry hits), shots your Wall of Sound or Resonant
  Guard turns back (4 each), shattering a Ring (10), and any explosion within 16 blocks (+15).
- **Fades** slowly after ten quiet seconds.
- **Spent** by a harmonic Resonant Note (30) and by Crescendo (all of it).

**Ringing.** Focused Shriek, Shatter Note, a braced Cry (Braced Voice) and Deep Echo leave enemies
**Ringing** for six seconds: two thin rings shiver round them like a struck bell. Your next sonic hit on a
Ringing enemy shatters the ring for **6 more damage** and **10 Resonance**.

## Abilities

| Key | Tier (Level) | Ability | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G | 1 (Level 1) | **Canary Cry** | **Tap** for a focused cry: damage and shove enemies in a narrow cone up to 44 blocks, and shatter glass in its path. **Hold** for the sustained scream, up to five seconds (6 extra stamina every half second). Your own cry **pushes you backward**: a shove for a tap, a steady push for the scream. **Crouch to brace**; in the air there is nothing to brace on. A Cry through your own Resonant Note breaks it **harmonic, for free**; the held scream **feeds** it instead (up to eight times, each a little wider and harder). *Pose:* arms thrown back by the voice; held, braced wide and shaking. |
| H | 2 (Level 3) | **Resonant Note** | Set a humming note where you aim, up to 20 blocks away. It plays a slow phrase for eight seconds. Hostile mobs within 16 blocks forget whoever they were hunting and walk to it, and they cannot pick a new target while it plays. **Press again** (free), or let it end, to break it on everything gathered there: 5 + 1.5 per tier damage, knocked away and slowed, glass, ice and leaves shattered. **Shift + press** (free) carries it once to where you aim, and the lured follow. With 30 Resonance the burst is **harmonic**: three blocks wider and half again as hard (the note glows gold when you have it). *Pose:* a plucked string; a closing fist to break it. |
| Z | 3 (Level 6) | **Wall of Sound** | A five-block-wide stave of standing sound waves just ahead, for six and a half seconds (seven and a half at tier 5). Shots that reach it turn back the way they came and become yours. Enemies in it are pushed away and hurt every half second. **Press again** (free) to **shove** it: it rolls twelve blocks forward over 0.8 seconds, hitting everything it passes for 4 + tier, carrying them and scooping up shots. Shift + key drops it. *Pose:* palms raised flat; a two-handed shove. |
| X | 4 (Level 10) | **Sonic Vault** | A cry at the ground behind you throws you away from your aim. Your next landing within ten seconds takes no damage. A real fall lands on a **shockwave** that hits and throws back everything within four blocks. **Press again in the air** (free) to **dive**: scream straight down and plunge onto a shockwave six blocks wide and half again as hard. *Pose:* arms flung up as you launch; pinned back in the dive. |
| V | 5 (Level 15) | **Crescendo** | **Hold** to build, up to three seconds: fronts close in on you and a stave climbs round your body. **Let go** (or reach three seconds) to release one wave in a wide cone. It reaches 16 to 32 blocks and deals 6 + up to 10 for building, + 1 per 10 Resonance, + tier. It spends all your Resonance, breaks your Resonant Note harmonic if it passes it, shatters glass, ice and leaves along its path, and leaves everything slowed and weakened. *Pose:* arms gathering in to the chest; flung wide and back on release. |

## Skill tree

The tree is built around the kit rather than stat bonuses: every node changes a move or ties two together.
Node IDs are unchanged, so earlier purchases carry over.

### Resonant Breaker (the voice as force)

| Node | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Braced Voice** | Crouch to brace as you tap the Cry and it focuses instead of throwing you back: a narrower cone that strikes 40% harder and leaves everything it hits Ringing. |
| **Focused Shriek** (active) | A hand cupped at the mouth and the whole voice through one point, up to 40 blocks: 9 + level damage and a shove, and the target is left Ringing. |
| **Feedback Loop** | A Cry or Crescendo through your own Wall of Sound comes out amplified: the Cry half again as hard and 12 blocks longer, the Crescendo 30% harder and 8 blocks longer. The wall rings out where it crosses. |
| **Shatter Note** (technique) | A finger-snap at a point up to 24 blocks away: glass, ice and leaves around it shatter, and enemies there take 4 and are left Ringing. |
| **Wider Shatter** | Shatter Note breaks a 5×5×5 area, and a Shatter Note near your own Note breaks it harmonic, for free. |
| **Resonant Breaker** | Shatter Note also breaks stone, dirt and wood (hardness up to 3), and stuns enemies that were already Ringing for a second. |

### Echo Cartographer (listening and luring)

| Node | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Perfect Pitch** | Enemies your Note lures glow for you through walls, and come to it faster. |
| **Dissonant Wave** (active) | A howl of clashing notes: visible enemies within 8 blocks take 3 damage, Weakness II and a strong slow. Enemies gathered at your Note take it twice and are thrown into the note. |
| **Siren Song** | The Note lures from 24 blocks, draws bigger creatures (up to 200 health) and plays four seconds longer. |
| **Echo Map** (technique) | A hand to the ear and a sonar sweep only you see: ores within 12 blocks glimmer for you. |
| **Deep Echo** | Echo Map reaches 20 blocks, and every enemy its sweep passes is left Ringing. |
| **Cartographer** | Echo Map also outlines every creature within 32 blocks and exposes the unseen. |

### Harmonist (holding the line)

| Node | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sympathetic Strings** | Friends' shots pass through your Wall of Sound and come out half again as fast and hard. |
| **Resonant Guard** (active) | Arms crossed, a low hum: Resistance for 5 seconds and a shell of standing waves 7 blocks across that turns incoming shots back (4 Resonance each). |
| **Sustain** | The Wall of Sound and Resonant Guard last half again as long, and Resonance doesn't fade while either stands. |
| **Quiet Zone** (technique) | A finger to the lips: a field of silence 6 blocks across for ten seconds. Mobs inside lose their targets and can't pick new ones. |
| **Long Silence** | The Quiet Zone lasts fifteen seconds. |
| **Harmonist** | Friends inside the Quiet Zone slowly heal. |

The three styles keep their effects and have new poses and drawings: **Resonance Cascade** (a conductor's
four downbeats as the waves stack), **Silence Dome** (palms pressed down; slate rings close inward) and
**Battle Hymn** (arms opened in song; a stave circles you and notes rise off it).

## Sound

The cry, the burst and the held scream are original synthesized sounds; no recordings are used.
Each layers a chorused, formant-shaped shriek (Canary) over a bowed cello-like drone (Emma), with a
pressure blast at the onset and glassy partials ringing out after. Rebuild them with
`python3 tools/generate-sonic-audio.py`.

## Evolved trial

**Catch 40 enemies in the burst of a Resonant Note** (up to 8 per burst count).

## For developers

- `sonic/SonicService` runs the kit; `sonic/SonicSkills` runs the branch actives and techniques;
  `sonic/SonicGeometry` holds the numbers and body anchors the server and the art share (the mouth, the
  hand, where a shoved wall is after *n* ticks).
- Every move is drawn by `client/effects/setpiece/SonicArt` from four primitives (`front`, `trace`,
  `staff`, `glyph`). Held things are re-sent short pieces: the scream every two ticks, the Note, Wall,
  Guard and Quiet Zone every ten, a Ringing mark every ten (bound to its target). The standing wall stops
  drawing the moment its push starts (`SetPieceClient.superseded`).
- The follow-ups use `SimpleAction.followUp`, so the second press is free and ignores the cooldown.
- Sonic is on the no-stat-bonus list in `SkillTreeService.bonus`.
