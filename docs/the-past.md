# The Last Signal

You wake in the Company’s Odessa stasis bunker 103 years after the city burned. You were there: on [the Last Day](the-last-day.md) you ran for this bunker when the light appeared, and Noah Bennet sealed you in a pod for your own good. At 6:14 PM Ted Sprague lost control downtown, and the stasis pods kept their occupants alive. The [Space-Time Manipulation awakening](time-manipulation-intro.md) lets its owner and nearby companions stand on the avenue, witness the explosion, and see the city run backwards before returning home.

## Follow the signal

1. Search the Overworld for the rare Company relay ruins. For a bearing, use the receiver in the bunker's records room after the lights come back. It records the live distress call and coordinates on **The Last Signal** page of the Journal (**J**).
2. Make your way to the relay ruins and follow the yellow markings. Activate the relay console to open the way to the stasis annex.
3. Use the isolator, then the coolant control. Once life support is stable, use **Mara** and **Ivo** to have them follow you, or sneak-use to carry them. Bring both from their pods to the extraction platform.
4. Use the extraction console once both sleepers reach the platform. Walk out of the station and return to the bunker. Speak with Mara and Ivo in the infirmary to hear their account and record their testimony in the Journal.

Primatech relay stations generate far apart in rings around the world origin, like strongholds. The campaign can be played with or without an awakened power, and you can find a station by exploring even if the bunker is disabled. The receiver's Journal entry shows the **X** and **Z** coordinates it found. After opening the annex, an electrical cast aimed near the isolator can cut the feed, and a cold cast aimed near the coolant valve can stabilize life support.

**Records**, **Dreams**, **The Way Back**, and **The Watchmaker** track optional discoveries, telepathic fragments, temporal milestones, and Sylar's story (the prisoner in Level 5, his watch list with its crossed-out names, his collection, and how often you have beaten him). The first time the bunker's receiver records the call, it also hands over Isaac Mendez's canvas of The Last Signal: a shareable painting of the sleepers that changes when the rescue succeeds.

## Existing worlds

Bunkers built before the Sublevel 4 rebuild (November 2026) keep their original rooms, and the players who sleep in them keep their pods; only new worlds get the rebuilt bunker and the Helix Ward. Existing bunkers receive the receiver and recovery rooms where the original fixtures are still in place; the old copper receiver becomes the radio console, and the ground round an existing ruined lobby is graded the same way. Look for relay stations in newly generated terrain. Previously earned story books, advancements, discovery rewards, and past outcomes remain in the save. Books from the earlier bunker layout and retired Hourglass Annex, Avenue at 6:14, and Night Three episodes remain valid discoveries when found. The game recovers players whose saves still place them in one of those visits.

## The first five minutes

New players first live [the Last Day](the-last-day.md), which ends with them sealed in a pod under Primatech. Then:

1. **STASIS FAILURE.** A new player wakes sealed inside a Cryochamber. A new bunker is on emergency
   power: every lamp is dark, and only the alarm beacons on its walls and ceilings turn, sweeping red
   beams across the hall. The facility alarm whoops in time with the red pulse, muffled through the
   lid, while the pod's own warning beeps quicken toward release.
2. **37,620 days.** The pod's counter: 103 years. Frost on the glass. The pod holds you while the revival protocol runs.
3. **You are awake.** The pod chimes and hisses open (about 5.5 seconds in), the waking's music begins, and you earn **Wake Up**.
   Once you are out of the pod and the alarm is over, the waking's music fades in and plays for as long as you are in
   the bunker, its stair or the ruined lobby at its top. Step out into the world and it fades away, and you earn
   **What happened...?**
4. **The power comes back.** The alarm holds until you step out of the pod. Then it cuts off, a breaker
   slams, and the bunker's lights click on in a wave from the generator, down the hall from its door to
   its far wall. The beacons keep turning.

Every new player wakes this way, in the pod assigned to them (the same number they were sealed in on the Last Day; never Pod 14, Claire Bennet's, which stands open and empty with her note inside), whose nameplate now carries their name
("SUBJECT 03 / name / REVIVAL IN PROGRESS", then "REVIVED"). Their waking cuts the main power again: the
lights go out, and everyone already in the bunker hears the alarm until the last sleeper is out of their
pod, when the power comes back for all of them.

A chest in the hall's aisle holds the orientation log. The receiver in Records gives directions to the relay ruins.

## Sublevel 4

The bunker is Primatech's Sublevel 4 as it stood a hundred and three years after the Last Day. Everything the years did to
it has a reason: the copper has gone green, most of all where water came in; the blast cracked the ceilings and brought
part of the stasis hall's down; a cave burst through the commons' wall, and the azalea in the commons' planter lived on
its water and grew into a tree; moss and roots creep out from it; some lights died, some glass broke, and cobwebs fill the
corners nobody cleaned. The Company kept its notes in chests. Open one to read what's inside (it counts as a discovery,
as reading a lectern does).

| Room | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Airlock** (behind the vault) | The vault's hazard-striped door, a decontamination arch with spray nozzles and a drain, emergency lockers, and the security booth: a desk, the security chest, and a maintenance hatch in its floor. Moss and vines have come in through the vault |
| **Commons** (the middle) | A two-storey rotunda: a gallery ring reached by two stairs, a chandelier of light boxes under a stepped ceiling, mess tables, a serving line and a reading lounge. A lush cave broke through its north-west wall: the planter's azalea is a tree now, grown up through the fallen gallery, and glow berries hang from the ceiling over it |
| **Stasis hall** (south of the commons) | Fourteen Cryochambers in arched niches, each with its copper pipes and nameplate, under catwalks on both walls joined at each end. The orientation log in the aisle, a grand stair up to the far balcony, and Isaac Mendez's painting of the explosion, Exhibit 1, high on the far wall over the analysts' station. Part of the ceiling came down over the west side, roots and dripstone hanging through the hole. Pod 14 stands open with Claire's note in a chest beside it (walking up to it tells you so), and a trail of old blood leads from it to containment. Every pod that is nobody's still holds one of Odessa's people from the Last Day, dead behind the glass under a red *DECEASED / CAUSE UNKNOWN* plate |
| **Records and comms** (east) | The signal receiver on the north desk: a Simple Radio receiver with a transmitter on it, a speaker and a microphone, the operator's chair. Filing cabinets, the records chest (bread, apples, torches), a planning table, a window onto the generator's core, and Noah Bennet's corner with his lockdown order |
| **Generator hall** (east) | The core, a column of light behind glass and copper; fourteen power cells (three still charged, one blown and scorched); a catwalk round three walls; the main breaker by the door. The power comes back from here |
| **Infirmary** (west) | Four screened cots with monitors, medical cabinets, a sink and an operating table under its lamp. Mara and Ivo recover here after the rescue |
| **Containment** (west) | Six barred cells and a watch desk. Cell 3 was Gabriel Gray's: the bars he unmade on the third night are still gone. Once his story begins, a hatch in its floor leads down to Level 5, where the Company kept him afterwards (see [Sylar](sylar.md)) |
| **Maintenance** (under the airlock) | Down the hatch in the security booth: a workbench, a map desk, a cot, storage, and Noah Bennet's own notes on Sublevel 4-R |
| **Sublevel 4-R, the Helix Ward** (behind the blast door off the east corridor) | See below |
| **The vault and the stair** (north of the airlock) | The vault door the Last Day's survivors came through, stuck open a hundred years, cracked and grown over with vines, moss and roots. Behind it a switchback stair climbs to a ruined lobby at world spawn, where it comes up through a railed slot in the floor under the old SUBLEVEL 4 sign. The lobby stands at the ground's middle height over its footprint, and the land round it is graded to meet its floor (cut back where a hill rose, filled where it fell away, trees cleared), so it sits in a clearing on the surface rather than in a pit. Glow lichen and glow berries light the way. (A world too shallow for the stair, such as a superflat one, gets a ladder to a hatch instead.) |

The bunker is built once, about 32 blocks below world spawn, the first time any player joins. Its books give more context about the Company, the sleepers, and Odessa.

## The Helix Ward

Behind a blast door off the east corridor, jammed since the blast brought the passage behind it down, is Sublevel 4-R:
one private pod on a frosted dais under a copper crown, the Formula bench, a specimen tank, and the office of Dr. A. Weir,
who sealed its one sleeper in at 6:11 PM on the Last Day and never left.

- **Who sleeps there.** When two or more new players live the Last Day together, one of them, drawn at random, is
  carried to the ward instead of the hall, given a dose, and sealed in its pod. Someone who lives the day alone is never
  chosen, and once the ward has had its sleeper nobody else ever goes there.
- **What it gives them.** A random power (any power nobody has yet, Space-Time included), dormant: it wakes in danger like
  any other. They are only told that something in their blood is waiting.
- **Waking there.** The usual waking (the alarm, the counter, the power cut and its return), alone, as *SUBJECT 15*,
  to the same music as everyone else. The blast door won't open from either side.
- **The way out.** Weir's journal says how: up the ladder to the mezzanine, where a copper duct (crouch to fit) runs through the rock and comes
  out on the stasis hall's east catwalk, over the others as they wake. Anyone in the hall can climb in the same way;
  Bennet's notes in maintenance tell where the vent is.

**Existing worlds:** players who have already played more than two minutes, or who already have an
awakened power, are not put in a pod. They get a chat message saying where the bunker is instead. A bunker built before
the rebuild keeps its rooms, ladder and hatch as they were built, receives compatible additions the first time someone
comes near its stasis hall, and has no Helix Ward.
Disable the spawn bunker with `bunker.enabled = false` in the common config.

## The Cryochamber

A two-block stasis pod. The bunker's pods are real Cryochambers, and players can craft their own.

- **Recipe:** iron ingots around glass (top), blue ice (middle) and one Helixite Shard (bottom).
- **Use an open pod** to step in. The door seals and the pod becomes **your respawn point**, like a
  bed that works at any time of day. If the pod is broken, you lose the respawn, just like a bed.
- **While sealed:** no ordinary damage, no hunger, and you heal half a heart every two seconds. You
  can't attack, use items or use powers. **Sneak** to step out.
- **Cryokinesis:** a Cryokinetic who sneak-uses a pod supercharges it for five minutes. It then
  heals four times faster and clears harmful effects and fire.
- **The Formula:** injecting yourself while sealed in a pod adds **+10%** to the success chance.
  The vial is the one item you can use inside.
- Only one sleeper per pod. It drops as an item when the bottom half is broken.


## Paintings

Isaac Mendez's paintings of the explosion and the black sun hang in his studio ruins. The painting of the explosion also hangs in the bunker as Exhibit 1. They are data-driven painting variants (`data/heroesevolved/painting_variant`) placed by blueprint markers. Older bunkers can still have exhibits from their original layout.

## Ruins on the surface

Up top, the world is scattered with what is left of the houses. They generate naturally in plains,
forests, savannas, taigas, snowy plains and deserts, and each has a chest to scavenge.

| Ruin | How common | What happened | What's inside |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Burnt house** | common | A timber-frame home that burned from the kitchen outward. Only the charred frame stands | Scavenged supplies, sometimes a survivor's note |
| **Collapsed house** | common | A brick house the blast wave pushed over. The west wall and chimney still stand | Scavenged supplies, sometimes a survivor's note |
| **The Bennet house** | rare | The fire took the east wing; a young oak grows there now. Claire's room survived upstairs, and the burnt-out car is still in the driveway | Claire's note for her dad, and his spare glasses |
| **Isaac Mendez's studio** | rare | A brick loft with a collapsed corner. His painting of the man who was the bomb hangs on the wall, with a study of the black sun opposite | Isaac's note about the painting that came true |
| **Primatech relay station** | stronghold-like rarity | A ruined Company relay above a buried stasis annex | The Last Signal rescue, starting at the relay console |

The survivor notes and the two story notes point back toward Odessa and the bunker. Find one with
`/locate structure heroesevolved:ruins_bennet_house` (or `ruins_isaac_studio`, `ruins_burnt_house`,
`ruins_collapsed_house`).

Ruins are drawn as blueprints (`blueprints/ruins_*.blueprint`) and exported to the structure files
the world generator uses:

```bash
python3 tools/blueprint.py to-nbt src/main/resources/data/heroesevolved/blueprints/ruins_bennet_house.blueprint --out src/main/resources/data/heroesevolved/structure/ruins/bennet_house.nbt
```

Photograph the four house and studio ruins in-game with:

```bash
./gradlew runClient --init-script tools/ruins-preview.init.gradle
```

The relay station has its own preview, `tools/signal-site-preview.init.gradle`.
