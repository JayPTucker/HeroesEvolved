# The Past: waking in the Odessa bunker

The city vision is not a warning about the future. In this world it already happened, centuries
ago, and every part of the story treats it that way: the bunker logs, the ruins, the villagers'
dreams, the Time Manipulation vision, the Hourglass Annex, the avenue at 6:14 and Sylar.

## The storyline: The Last Day

You wake centuries after Odessa burned. The books tell you what happened, and your powers let you
go back to it. One rule holds it together: **the light can't be stopped; the people under it can
be saved.** Hiro tried to stop the bomb twice, which is why his note says he was "too late, again".
That is also why the surface stays in ruins however much a player changes.

| When | What happened | Where players meet it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | The Company collects powered people. Isaac Mendez paints the bomb, and a canvas of sleepers waking. Hiro warns them. | *Lockdown Order 7*, *I Painted It*, *The Men in Suits*, Exhibits 4 and 9 |
| The Last Day, afternoon | Ted Sprague asks the Company for help. They take him below the city, behind a door with an hourglass on it. | Telepathy memory 6 |

| 6:10 PM | Noah Bennet opens the Company's street door at the south end of the avenue and holds it. A man in a dark coat walks up the avenue toward the glow. | **The Avenue at 6:14**, the finale (*Follow omen* once you hold the key) |
| 6:14 PM | Sprague loses control downtown. Bennet closes his door. Gray is on the avenue, watching, unless someone kept him from it; that night men in suits take him below. | The Time Manipulation vision; memories 7 to 13; *The Door at 6:14*, *Addendum: G. Gray* |
| That night | Bennet seals Sublevel 4 with Claire in Pod 14. | *Lockdown Order 7*, *Dad*, *For Claire* |
| Night three | Gray reaches Pod 14 and takes Claire's regeneration. He stops aging. | *Containment: G. Gray*, the blood trail |

| Now | The pods fail one at a time. You wake, and the ticking starts. Why you: Isaac painted you. | The intro; *Why You* |

The Eclipse is the explosion's echo. It is why powers wake, and why villagers dream the last
memories of people who died on the avenue (the telepathy memories, now in the Journal as
**Dreams of the Last Day**). Whoever owns Radiation now carries Sprague's heat, so the present has
stakes too: if Sylar learns it, Odessa happens again.

### Following it

Press **J**. The Journal opens on a **Next lead**: the one thing to look for now, chosen from the
records you have found, whether your power has woken, the Annex and the avenue. In order, the leads
are: the orientation log, Bennet's lockdown order, the ruins (Isaac's studio or the Bennet house),
waking your power, Gray's containment file, Hiro's note, the hidden office under the red rug, a
cartographer's canvas, the Annex rescue, the avenue, and Gray if he saw the light. Then the lead counts
the records still missing in the bunker and in the ruins, and finally the people still left on the
avenue. Leads only point; nothing is locked behind them except the avenue itself, which needs Hiro's key.

Below it, **Recovered records** lists every book and note found, in story order, out of the 17 the
world holds: 13 hidden in the bunker and the ruins, and 4 that only exist once you change the past.

**Follow omen** goes to the newest omen: the Annex until you hold Hiro's key, then the avenue. Hold
**Shift** while clicking it to walk into the Annex instead. **Leave the past** brings you home from
either.

The HUD names where you are in time: **PAST · ODESSA, THE LAST DAY** in the city (the vision and the
avenue), **PAST · HOURGLASS ANNEX, 5:50 PM** in the Annex, and **PAST** in a Walk the Past recording.
Everyone there sees it, with or without a power. Time Manipulation owners see **PRESENT** everywhere else.

### The Avenue at 6:14

The finale. Hiro's key says it plainly: the light at 6:14 is fixed, nobody under it is. **Follow omen**
takes you to the city from the Time vision at 6:10 PM, standing in front of the Company's street door at
the south end of the avenue. Up to eight players run one evening together; latecomers join the evening
already in progress.

- **Bennet's door.** Noah Bennet holds the door of Primatech Paper until 6:14, not a second longer.
  Anyone through it when the light falls lives.
- **Twenty-four people** stand on the avenue and the streets crossing it, staring north at the glow:
  a baker, a bus driver, three children, a doorman. Each has a name above their head. **Use** someone
  and they follow the nearest runner; **sneak + use** to carry them (Flight, Superspeed and the rest
  all help). Followers who can see the door from where they stand make for it themselves.
- **Mara and Ivo**, alive because of you, stand by the door. Every 45 seconds one of them shouts to
  the nearest person still standing within 48 blocks, who runs for it.
- **Gabriel Gray** walks up the avenue toward the light, unhurried: a little over two minutes if
  nobody stops him. If he gets within 18 blocks of Sprague before 6:14, he sees how the heat works.
  Every power and blow works on him, but the past won't let him be hurt: each hit staggers him and
  pushes him back, at most one every two seconds, and three turn him around for good. Holding him in
  stopped time or pushing him back until 6:14 also counts. The choice is yours: chase Gray, or carry
  one more person.
- **Ted Sprague** glows at the north end. Nobody can reach him; the heat drives you back.
- **Every power works** here, unlike in the vision, but nobody can break or place blocks, and lethal harm
  throws you back to the present instead of killing you. The action bar shows the clock, how many
  are safe, and what Gray is doing.

At 6:14 Bennet closes the door, the light falls exactly as in the vision, and everyone is pulled home
to where they left. What comes back with you:

| Outcome | In the present |
| --- | --- |
| Anyone saved | *The Door at 6:14* with their names, *Why You*, and the **6:15** advancement |
| All 24 saved | **Everyone Home** (a challenge) |
| Gray kept from the light | *Addendum: G. Gray* and **He Never Saw It**. For the whole world: Sylar can never take Radiation from its owner. If he already had, he loses it ("He never did"). |
| Gray saw the light | Nothing new, and the lead sends you back for him |

The best evening is what counts: a replay can add names, never remove them, and once Gray is kept away
he stays kept away. Everything is idempotent: discoveries, advancements and the Sylar change.

Only one evening runs at a time, and never while someone's Time vision is using the city (each waits
for the other). An evening lives in memory: logging out, dying or a server stop sends runners home by
their saved ticket, and the street, Bennet's door and everyone on it are put back afterward.
`AvenueGameTests` plays a whole evening with a real player: following, the door, turning Gray, the
light, coming home, the records and the Sylar change.

## What happened

The Company (Primatech) kept powered people in **Cryochambers** in a stasis wing deep under its
Odessa site. Then the painting came true: Ted Sprague lost control downtown, the city burned, and
Noah Bennet sealed the bunker. Above it, centuries passed and nature took the surface back. Below,
the pods kept their sleepers until the power cells began to fail. Now each pod opens when someone
arrives to wake in it.

Gabriel Gray, Sylar, was held in the same wing. During the lockdown he took the regeneration
sleeper's ability, unmade his cell's bars, and walked out. He does not age. He has been waiting for
the sleepers to wake, which is why the ticking follows new players.

A note in unfamiliar handwriting, from Hiro, says the past can still be changed, and points to
"the annex beneath the hourglass". His key there leads to the avenue at 6:14, and his last note
says why you. See [The storyline](#the-storyline-the-last-day) above.

## The first five minutes

1. **STASIS FAILURE.** A new player joins sealed inside a Cryochamber under red emergency lights.
   An alarm pulses, the screen washes red, and the old city siren sounds faintly.
2. **How long were you asleep?** Frost on the glass. The pod holds you while the revival protocol runs.
3. **You are awake.** The pod hisses open (about 5.5 seconds in) and you earn **Wake Up**.

A lectern in the middle of the chamber holds the orientation log. From there:

| Room | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Stasis hall** | A 12-high hall: 14 Cryochambers under copper galleries, red emergency lights, hanging lamps, the orientation log on the console. A lush cave has broken through the south-east ceiling, and its glow berries light that end. A blood trail runs from Claire Bennet's pod (14) to Sylar's cell |
| **The west gallery** | Climb the stairs at the south end. Exhibit 9 hangs there: Isaac Mendez's painting of someone waking in a pod |
| **Records room** | Noah Bennet's lockdown order, the last researcher's "Day 2,190" log, filing cabinets, Exhibit 4 (Isaac's *Black Sun*), and a chest with Hiro's note, bread, apples and torches |
| **Lab** (east of the corridor) | A working Centrifuge and Synthesis Station, a cracked specimen tube, the Helixite assay log, and a chest with 1 Helixite Shard, 2 Empty Syringes and 3 glass bottles |
| **The cell** (west of the corridor) | Sylar's containment file, the cage whose bars he unmade, his watchmaker's bench, and the end of the blood trail. You'll hear ticking the first time you step inside |
| **Generator hall** (east of records) | A cold reactor, 14 power cells (3 still lit), a catwalk |
| **Security** (west of records) | A barrier, the guard post, lockers, Company gear, and a guard's *Shift Notes* about an ugly red rug |
| **Sublevel 4-B** (secret) | Under the red rug in security is a trapdoor. Noah Bennet's hidden office is at the bottom of the ladder: the family photo, his letter *For Claire*, and a chest with Claire's donated blood (a Formula catalyst), 2 Helixite Shards, a syringe and a spyglass |
| **Shaft** | A ladder up to a spruce trapdoor on the surface, directly at world spawn |

The bunker is built once, about 32 blocks below world spawn, the first time any player joins. It
has six recovered logs in total; read in order, they tell the whole story.

**Existing worlds:** players who have already played more than two minutes, or who already have an
awakened power, are not put in a pod. They get a chat message saying where the bunker is instead.
Turn the whole feature off with `bunker.enabled = false` in the common config.

## The Cryochamber

A two-block stasis pod. The bunker's pods are real Cryochambers, and players can craft their own.

- **Recipe:** iron ingots around glass (top), blue ice (middle) and one Helixite Shard (bottom).
- **Use an open pod** to step in. The door seals and the pod becomes **your respawn point**, like a
  bed that works at any time of day. If the pod is broken, you lose the respawn, just like a bed.
- **While sealed:** no ordinary damage, no hunger, and you heal half a heart every two seconds. You
  can't attack, use items or use powers. **Sneak** to step out.
- **Cryokinesis:** a Cryokinetic who sneak-uses a pod supercharges it for five minutes. It then
  heals four times faster and clears harmful effects and fire.
- **The Formula:** injecting yourself while sealed in a pod adds **+10%** to the success chance.
  The vial is the one item you can use inside.
- Only one sleeper per pod. It drops as an item when the bottom half is broken.

## Paintings

Four custom paintings tie the places together: *The Man Who Was the Bomb* and *The Black Sun* in Isaac's
studio, Exhibits 4 and 9 that the Company took to the bunker, and the Bennet family photo in Sublevel 4-B.
They are data-driven painting variants (`data/heroesevolved/painting_variant`), drawn by
`tools/generate-paintings.py`, and hung from blueprints with `@painting:<variant>:<facing>` markers.

## Ruins on the surface

Up top, the world is scattered with what is left of the houses. They generate naturally in plains,
forests, savannas, taigas, snowy plains and deserts, and each has a chest to scavenge.

| Ruin | How common | What happened | What's inside |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Burnt house** | common | A timber-frame home that burned from the kitchen outward. Only the charred frame stands | Scavenged supplies, sometimes a survivor's note |
| **Collapsed house** | common | A brick house the blast wave pushed over. The west wall and chimney still stand | Scavenged supplies, sometimes a survivor's note |
| **The Bennet house** | rare | The fire took the east wing; a young oak grows there now. Claire's room survived upstairs, and the burnt-out car is still in the driveway | Claire's note for her dad, and his spare glasses |
| **Isaac Mendez's studio** | rare | A brick loft with a collapsed corner. His painting of the man who was the bomb hangs on the wall, with a study of the black sun opposite | Isaac's note about the painting that came true |

The survivor notes and the two story notes point back toward Odessa and the bunker. Find one with
`/locate structure heroesevolved:ruins_bennet_house` (or `ruins_isaac_studio`, `ruins_burnt_house`,
`ruins_collapsed_house`).

Ruins are drawn as blueprints (`blueprints/ruins_*.blueprint`) and exported to the structure files
the world generator uses:

```bash
python3 tools/blueprint.py to-nbt src/main/resources/data/heroesevolved/blueprints/ruins_bennet_house.blueprint --out src/main/resources/data/heroesevolved/structure/ruins/bennet_house.nbt
```

Photograph all four in-game with:

```bash

```
