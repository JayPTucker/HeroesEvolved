# The End portal: two offerings

Twelve Eyes of Ender complete the frame, but the center stays dark. The eyes need the gift of an awakened person at
**Power Level 16 or higher**. This is your Power Level, not your experience level.

Stand on a filled frame, look into the center, and **hold an ability key**. Any power can answer, including a support
power. The key channels into the eye instead of casting its usual move. Begin with at least 95% stamina. Over eight
seconds your hands reach down toward the eye, your power streams into it, and your stamina drains completely.

| Playing together | Playing alone |
| --- | --- |
| Two eligible players stand on touching frames along the same side and channel together. Each spends one bar. Their powers can be the same. | Charge one frame first. It remembers your offering for two minutes. Recover your stamina, then charge a touching frame on that same side. You spend two bars, one after the other. |

The remembered offering is a floating iris of light in your power's colour. It is a memory in the frame, not a second
player, and works on both singleplayer worlds and servers. Opposite frames and frames across a corner cannot form a
pair. Charging the same eye twice does not count.

Releasing the original key, stepping off the frame, looking away, losing your power, being suppressed, dying or
leaving cancels an unfinished channel. Stamina already spent stays spent; a completed offering keeps its own timer.
An incomplete frame or an obstructed center cannot be opened. Interrupted offerings are forgotten when the world
closes; an opened portal is saved as ordinary End portal blocks and remains open.

## What it looks and sounds like

The dormant frame breathes with a faint teal outline. As you channel, both palms turn toward the eye: coloured streams
unspool from the hands, collect in its floating iris, and send pulses around the twelve eyes. Electricity forks, fire
curls, water flows in braided ribbons, pressure and sound travel in rings, and the quieter gifts weave fine strands
of light. Each offering leaves a slowly turning, fading imprint above its eye.

When two neighboring offerings meet, their threads cross the center. A dark membrane stretches between them, bends
inward, and opens in a widening iris. Pale teal edges and violet depths settle into the familiar starry portal, with
an ascending chime and the portal's opening sound. Entry is voluntary: recover before stepping in.

An existing open portal stays usable. Power Level is checked when giving an offering; traveling through an opened
portal does not charge stamina or repeat the ritual.
