# Classes: Offense, Defense and Support

Every power can be played three ways. On the **Class** page of the hero menu (press **P**, or click **Skills** in your inventory, then **CLASS**) you choose one class for your power:

| Class | Perk (always on) |
| --- | --- |
| **Offense** | Your attacks deal 10% more damage. |
| **Defense** | You take 12% less damage. |
| **Support** | Friends within 12 blocks regenerate, and so do you while one is near. |

Each class also gives you its **Ultimate**, the biggest thing your power can do. A class costs **3 skill points** and opens at **Level 6**. You keep one class at a time. Choosing another class gives the old one's points back, needs ten seconds out of combat, and puts the new Ultimate on whatever key the old one was on. **RESPEC** clears your class along with everything else.

Ultimates appear on the **Loadout** page. Assign them to any of your eight keys or a favorite position on the ability wheel. A new class fills an unused wheel position if one is available. "Friends" means you, your own pets, and players on your team (or, with PvP off, any player). Ultimates never break or place blocks.

## Ultimates

An Ultimate is rare: it costs a lot of stamina and waits a long time before you can use it again.

| Kind | Stamina | Waits | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Offense** | 58 | 2½ minutes | Takes the foe in front of you (within 8 to 14 blocks, depending on the power) and plays out a short scene on it: the foe is held the whole time and can't hurt you, the screen narrows to a letterbox and the camera follows the action. It lands 20 to 25 damage on that foe, and a splash on anyone near the finish. |
| **Defense** | 50 | 2 minutes | Opens with a short hero shot, then lasts 10 to 15 seconds while you fight on: a shell, a stance or a trick that turns aside shots and blows, or keeps you alive. |
| **Support** | 52 | 2¼ minutes | Opens with a short hero shot, then lasts up to 15 seconds for everyone near you: healing, shelter, speed or strength. |

An Offense Ultimate needs a foe in front of you, in sight; with nobody there, it is refused and costs nothing. Nothing else can be cast while an Ultimate plays. Dying, logging out, changing dimension or having your power dampened ends it at once: a held foe is let go and gets its mind back, and you are never left inside a wall.

You can turn the cinematic camera off under **Settings** in the hero menu (**Ultimate camera: Your own eyes**); the scene still plays, seen from your own eyes.

## How they are drawn

Ultimates are **set pieces** in each power's own art (`client/effects/setpiece/<Power>Art`): shapes, not particles, leaving the visible hand, with the server keeping the drawing's schedule. Offense Ultimates also move the camera through a few key positions (`UltimateCameraPayload`, drawn by `client/UltimateCamera`) and close letterbox bars over the screen.

To photograph every Ultimate with foes and a friend present, from its own camera and from the side, run `./gradlew runClient --init-script tools/ultimate-preview.init.gradle` (add `-Ponly=sunfall,phase_blur` or a power's id for a few). The screenshots land in `build/ultimate-preview/screenshots/`. Gameplay is checked by `ClassAbilityGameTests` and `UltimateGameTests`.
