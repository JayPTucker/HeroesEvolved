# Classes: Offense, Defense and Support

Every power can be played three ways. On the **Class** page of the hero menu (press **P**, or click **Skills** in your inventory, then **CLASS**) you choose one class for your power:

| Class | Perk (always on) |
| --- | --- |
| **Offense** | Your attacks deal 10% more damage. |
| **Defense** | You take 12% less damage. |
| **Support** | Friends within 12 blocks regenerate, and so do you while one is near. |

Each class also gives you its **class ability**, a showpiece with its own animation (see the table below). A class costs **3 skill points** and opens at **Level 6**. You keep one class at a time. Choosing another class gives the old one's points back, needs ten seconds out of combat, and puts the new ability on whatever key the old one was on. **RESPEC** clears your class along with everything else.

Class abilities are learned skills like any other: they appear on the **Loadout** page and the ability wheel, and you can put them on any of your eight keys. "Friends" means you, your own pets, and players on your team (or, with PvP off, any player). Area abilities never break or place blocks, and their searches are capped.

## How they are drawn

Class abilities are **set pieces**: real geometry drawn on every nearby client as a pure function of time, the way the vision's explosion over Odessa is. They include lumpy billows of smoke, fire and dust, glass and lattice shells, rings and ribbons of light, lightning, crystal shards and rock slabs. The server sends one small packet (`SetPiecePayload`) when the piece starts and does the gameplay on the same ticks the piece shows it, so what you see and what happens agree. The drawing code lives in `client/effects/setpiece/`. Induced Radioactivity's **Critical Mass** and **Ground Zero** both use `MiniBlast`, the vision's blast at any size: the flash, the fireball rolling up into a mushroom cloud, the condensation dome and the ring of dust.

To photograph every piece on a stage in a flat world, run `./gradlew runClient --init-script tools/style-preview.init.gradle`. The screenshots land in `build/style-preview/screenshots/styles/`. Gameplay is checked by `ClassAbilityGameTests`.
