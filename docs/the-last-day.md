# The Last Day

Every new player's story starts on the Last Day in Odessa, 103 years before they wake in the bunker under spawn. You
live the evening the city ended. You wake in your own apartment, walk its streets and run when the light appears.
Noah Bennet seals you in a pod "for your own good". Then the [usual waking](the-past.md#the-first-five-minutes) begins,
in the pod with the same number.

When you emerge from a newly built bunker in the present, you can retrace these same streets through Odessa's ruins.
The buildings keep their original locations; the surviving rooms hold scavenged supplies and civilian journals from
the first days after the blast. See [the surface ruins](the-past.md#the-first-five-minutes).

## What happens

1. **Your apartment.** You wake in the **Odessa Arms**, a brick walk-up a block west of Primatech Paper, with a fire
   escape up its front and a water tank on its roof. The caption reads *Odessa · 6:02 PM*, and your name is on the plate
   by your door. There are twelve apartments on three floors, each with a bed under the window, a kitchen, a table and a
   radio. A wooden stairwell winds down from every floor to the lobby: two short flights and a half landing a storey,
   round an open well with a rail, under a skylight. With more than twelve new players at once, they
   share: the emptiest apartment takes the next person, and the plate lists who lives there.
   - **Meeting in the lobby.** Your apartment door is open; the building's street doors are not. A **thread of light**
     (see below) leads you down the stairwell. Everyone who woke with you meets in the lobby downstairs, and each of you
     says you're ready: coming down puts a line in chat with an **[I'm ready]** button, or just type *ready* (or *I'm
     ready*) in chat. Saying it upstairs doesn't count, and going back up takes your word back.
   - In a group, once you're ready you're told to **wait for your friends** (and how many are still getting ready), and
     the others see who is ready (*Sam is ready (2 of 3)*). The street doors open the moment the last of you says it.
     Trying the doors early gives you the button again, or says who you're still waiting on.
   - **Nothing starts on a timer.** If someone never readies (they went AFK), the ones who are ready wait two minutes,
     then get a **[Go without them]** button. Clicking it opens the doors; whoever is still upstairs can follow when they
     come down. Alone, you just click the button and go.
2. **The evening.** The doors open on an ordinary evening, three minutes long, to quiet, uneasy music: low strings, a slow
   pulse, a few far-off piano notes.
   - **An errand.** You're sent for groceries at the **City Market**, a grocery up the avenue a block south of Odessa
     Square, with produce out on the sidewalk, aisles of shelves and a checkout. The thread of light leads up the avenue,
     in at the shop's door and down to the **checkout counter**. Step up to the counter and the bag is yours; then the
     thread leads you back out front to wait for the others.
   - **Doors.** Wooden doors (and gates) all over the city open and shut by hand, as anywhere else: right-click them. The
     day puts every one back as it was when it ends. Only the Arms' street doors stay shut until everyone is ready.
   - **Then it happens.** Twenty seconds after the last of you has been to the counter, the light appears up the avenue.
     If someone never gets there, the evening still ends when its three minutes are up.
   - The whole city is open to walk, out to the suburbs and the prairie; nothing turns you back at the edge of town.
   - Odessa's people chat on the sidewalks, look in shop windows and walk round the block. It's unhurried: a
     conversation every thirteen seconds or so near you, with a breath between lines.
   - Their talk has hints of what's coming: Ted Sprague looked sick today, Isaac Mendez painted the street on fire, and
     there are guards on every door at Primatech.
   - Two Company agents stand outside Primatech's lobby, their TaCZ pistols **holstered on their hips**: nothing has
     happened yet. They draw the moment the fight starts.
3. **The light.** Far up the avenue, in the middle of the street, **Ted Sprague** is down on his knees, glowing.
   - Nobody runs: people stop and stare, and wonder aloud. Shock waves roll off him every few seconds, in Induced
     Radioactivity's colours: a white-hot ring racing out over the street with a sickly green one behind it, a dome of
     heat haze and a skirt of dust, each one bigger and closer to the last. You hear each thud a moment after you see it.
   - **The fight.** Peter Petrelli streaks in high over the city from the south, trailing condensation, with a boom you
     feel; Sylar shows himself over Kirby Plaza in a burst of dark smoke, and they meet. The music changes as they do:
     driving drums, hi-hats and low strings come up over the evening's music. Four passes over the plaza, lightning and
     pressure fronts and telekinetic grips, all drawn as the powers themselves (you see Peter and Sylar as themselves,
     however far off). Then Sylar grips Peter from across the sky and **hurls him down the avenue** to the market, and the
     fight comes right over your head: Peter's lightning, Sylar thrown back, and then Sylar tears a **chunk out of a
     building** and flings it at Peter. Peter dodges. It smashes into the market's front instead, and **stone and glass
     come down on the sidewalk**. A second chunk, the other way, hits the building across the street. Peter tackles
     Sylar, hurls him back up the avenue into the dark tower by the plaza in a burst of dust, flies back and dives into the
     light beside Ted.
   - **Someone is trapped.** The rubble lands on the people standing on the sidewalks: one for every two of you
     (*Marisol, a cashier*; *Mr. Pruitt*; *Gus, a welder*...). They're on the ground under it, calling for help.
   - **The wave.** A breath after Peter reaches Ted, the big one comes: a wall of pressure and dust that crosses the
     whole city. **You hear it coming**: about four seconds before it reaches you a rumble starts under the street,
     deep at first, then a roar of air and grinding stone on top, and the ground shakes harder and harder. For the last
     second and a half **your head is turned to it**, whatever you were looking at, and held there (the mouse can't turn
     it away), so you see the wall come. **The windows it passes burst** in one great crash: about half the glass, pane by
     pane, so every window is left jagged, with the shards lying glittering on the sidewalks below. It throws you flat on
     your back: the world goes white, then **black for about three seconds**. You're out cold, but you can still hear the
     screaming, slowed, wavering and muffled, swelling with your pulse. Twice your eyes almost open on a slit of light and
     fall shut again. Then your **ears ring** (a high tone, everything else muffled under it, slowly coming back), and you
     blink, trying to wake up, the sky swimming, before you can get to your feet. Odessa's people go down too.
   - **The city comes apart.** As the wave goes by, roof edges and pieces of facade are torn loose and fall into the
     street, and broken stone, slabs and gravel are thrown across it. When you get up the air is thick with dust (it thins,
     but never quite clears), and the buildings keep giving way: every second or two a section of some facade near you
     cracks and comes down with a roar of dust and a tremor. Smoke climbs from where the worst of it fell. Primatech, its
     doors and its stair are left clear, and so are the people under the rubble.
   - **The sirens** are the first thing you hear through the ringing. Now the vault under Primatech is open (it was shut
     all evening; you can find it if you explore), the agents call people in (*"Bring them with you! Down the stairs in
     the lobby!"*), and Odessa's people run for it: in through Primatech's doors and **down the stair, the same way you
     go**, through the vault and the corridor into the stasis hall, where they wait along the walls in front of the pods.
   - **Dig them out.** The thread of light leads you to whoever is trapped. Their legs are buried in **gravel**, more of it
     heaped on top: it breaks by hand in under a second and drops nothing, and what's heaped above slides down into the hole,
     so you dig a few times. When it's all off their legs they're free, but they can't stand. Then **sneak and right-click
     them, or the ground beside them**, to lift them into your arms (trying while they're still buried tells you to dig
     first), and carry them to Primatech: through its doors, where a railed slot
     in the lobby floor opens on a **wide stair** down. It goes down east, turns at a landing, and comes back west to the
     vault door. Set them down (or get darted) and they lie where they are until someone lifts them again.
   - You have about **two and a half minutes** from the light. It grows the whole time and surges eight times after the
     wave, closer and closer toward the end. Each surge whites out the sky and shakes the ground a little harder, but you
     never see it go off.
4. **Too slow.** Anyone still outside when time runs out is darted by an agent behind them. They see one last, biggest
   surge, then nothing, and are carried down and sealed in a pod. Anyone still under the rubble, or left on the street,
   is lost (*Not everyone under the rubble got out*). Nobody holds the group up.
5. **The safe house.** Past the turn in the stair you're safe, and there's no going back up, and so is whoever you
   carried (*"You came back for me. You didn't have to."*); they're laid by the wall in the stasis hall. The vault leads
   into the stasis bunker as it was that evening: lights on, nothing broken, every pod open and waiting, and Company
   guards at the vault and along the corridor. The music falls to a murmur down here and fades away as the scene begins.
   **The vault shuts too soon.** Once the last of you is through (or after ten seconds), it slams shut, with people still
   on the stair: always a few of them. You hear them pounding on the steel, and their voices through it in chat
   (*"Let us in! Please, let us in!"*, *"There's still people out here! Open the door!"*), and the guards yell at you to
   keep moving (*"That's all we can take! Seal it!"*, *"You! Don't stop! Move down the hall, now!"*; again a few seconds
   later if you're still standing by the door). The pounding fades as you go down the corridor. In the stasis hall,
   Odessa's people stand along the walls, and Claire Bennet is begging her father:
   > Dad, I don't want to go, please! I can stop this!

   When everyone is in the hall (the scene waits up to 30 seconds for stragglers in the corridor), the scene plays out
   slowly, a line every five seconds or so, so there's time to read each one: Claire begs, Noah tells her to get in,
   she says she can heal and that Peter is her uncle, and Noah answers that nobody walks away from what's coming. He walks
   her to **Pod 14**, shoves her in and seals it (she stands inside with both palms on the glass), rests a hand on the
   glass, and promises to come back for her. Then he
   turns to you:
   > I know you don't understand. This is for your own good.

   **Nobody leaves the hall** while this happens: anyone who steps out is brought back to just inside the door. Noah draws
   a **Company stun gun** (a stubby yellow-and-black gun with a cartridge on the front, not a firearm) and **walks up to
   you**.
   - **On your own,** he does it himself: *"Hold still. It'll be over in a second."* He stops an arm's length away,
     raises it, and fires.
   - **With friends,** he calls in the guards: *"Guards! Take them down. Gently."* Each guard in the bunker draws a stun
     gun, walks up to one of you (following you if you back away), raises it and fires; with more of you than guards,
     they work through you in turn.
6. **The pods.** You come to sealed in your own pod, labelled with your name, as a short music cue falls away. **Every
   other pod in the hall** (except Claire's) **holds one of Odessa's people** now, sealed in, their name on the plate
   (*SUBJECT 03 / J. PRUITT / STASIS / SEALED*): whoever you carried down first, then the people who came down with you,
   and if there weren't enough of them, people who came down before you did. Pods that belong to players who lived an
   earlier Last Day are left for them. If two or
   more of you lived the day together and the Helix Ward is still waiting for its one sleeper, one of you, drawn at
   random, comes to somewhere else: a small white ward on your own, a needle's sting fading from your arm (see
   [the Helix Ward](the-past.md#the-helix-ward)).
   - Screaming far above, and the ground shaking harder and harder.
   - Claire, through the glass of Pod 14: *"Uncle Peter... please..."*
   - One terrible blow through the rock. Every light in the hall dies, then stutters back (about a third stay dark), and
     stone breaks from the ceiling and comes down the aisle in a haze of dust. Then silence.
   - The pod hums on. Frost creeps across your sight, and everything goes black.
   - **103 years later...**, over a slow swell of music. The title stays up for the whole of it, and you wake only once
     the music has finished (waking takes you to another dimension, which stops every sound).
7. **Waking.** The bunker under spawn wakes you in the pod with the same number (*STASIS FAILURE*, the alarm, and the
   pod counter reading 37,620 days), and the story carries on as [The Last Signal](the-past.md).

The final blast is heard once. Falling masonry has its own stone and gravel sounds; reaching shelter does not replay the explosion.

### The thread of light

A kind of clairvoyance shows you the way: a thin, pale blue-white thread of light at waist height, as if you could already
see where you'll walk. It follows the real route (down stairwells, round corners, through doors you can open), prefers the street to shortcuts through unrelated rooms, starts a couple
of steps ahead of you, is brightest near you and thins away into the distance, with slow pulses running along it toward
the goal, where it gathers into a soft glow on the ground. Each player has their own, and it changes as the day does:

| When | It leads to |
| --- | --- |
| You wake upstairs | the lobby |
| The evening | inside the City Market to the checkout counter, then back out front to wait |
| The light, before the rubble falls | nowhere: just watch |
| Someone is trapped | the nearest one still under the rubble, then the nearest one freed and waiting to be lifted |
| You're carrying someone, or nobody needs help and the vault is open | Primatech's stair, then down it to the vault |
| Down in the bunker | the stasis hall |

Everything the story says goes to chat, one line per beat, and nothing is laid over the scene:
- **Where you are:** a caption line with a grey subtitle (*Odessa · 6:02 PM*, *Something is wrong downtown.*).
- **What to do:** a line starting », written once when it changes (*go down to the lobby*, *dig them out*, *pick them
  up*, *carry them down*, *get to the safe house*).
- **Who speaks:** Noah, Claire, the agents and the people you dig out as `<Name> line` (no floating text). Someone still
  under the rubble cries for help in a bubble over their head every few seconds, as the crowd's chatter does.
- **What happens to you:** one line each for the dart, the stun gun and the pod.

The only text on screen is the vanilla title card *103 years later...*, and the *PAST* label in the corner, which hides
while the screen is black. Odessa's people still talk in small speech bubbles in the street: their chatter is the city,
not the story.

### Claire and Peter

Peter Petrelli, her uncle, is the one up there with Ted, and Claire says so: in the safe house while the others come down
(*"I can help Peter. He'll die if I'm not there!"*, *"He's my uncle, Dad."*), when the vault shuts, all through her
father's scene, and once more through the glass of her pod. Each line is said once.

### How they look

Our own skins, drawn to be recognisable without copying anyone's photo or costume:

- **Peter Petrelli:** dark hair to the chin, parted and falling over one eye; all in black: an open jacket over a tee,
  dark jeans and boots.
- **Sylar:** short black hair, heavy brows, stubble; a long charcoal coat over a grey collared shirt, and a watch.
- **Ted Sprague:** a brown trucker cap with a mesh back, shaggy brown hair and a full beard; an open red-brown plaid
  flannel over a brown tee, jeans and work boots.
- **Noah Bennet:** short auburn hair, a navy suit, a pale blue shirt and dark tie, and his horn-rimmed glasses (below).
- **Claire Bennet:** long blonde hair and a red and white cheerleading uniform.

### Noah's glasses

Noah wears a real pair of **Horn-Rimmed Glasses**, an item drawn on his face, not painted on his skin. Players can wear
them too (use them, or put them in the head slot): a spare pair is in the security chest of a newly built bunker, and
they can be crafted from two sticks, two glass panes and an iron nugget (sticks in the top corners, panes either side of
the nugget below).

## In the present

- **Pod 14 is open and empty.** Its plate reads *SUBJECT 14 / C. BENNET / RELEASED*, and nobody is ever assigned to
  it. Frost still clouds its glass, clawed through from the inside, with a star of cracks where she struck it. **Walking
  up to it** (within three blocks) tells you so in chat, once: *Pod 14 stands open, its frost clawed through from the
  inside. Someone left a note beside it.* **Claire's note** waits in a chest beside it (on a lectern in bunkers built
  before the rebuild; put back if someone takes it): she woke decades early, healed through the cold, sat with the
  sleepers she couldn't safely wake, is sorry for what her father did, and went up to find out what was left.
- **The other pods still hold Odessa's people.** Every hall pod that isn't Claire's and isn't any player's has a body in
  it, head bowed, behind shut glass, under a red plate: *SUBJECT 03 / J. PRUITT / DECEASED / CAUSE UNKNOWN* (the same
  names as on the Last Day). The pod won't open on them (*The seal holds. Whoever is inside has been dead a long time.*),
  and they can't be traded with. When a new player is given one of those pods, it is emptied for them before they wake
  in it.
- **The ones the vault was shut on** never got in: skulls lie on the landing outside the vault door, and here and there
  on the stair's landings all the way up.
- **Lockdown Order 7** in Records is Noah's order from that evening. Everyone on the Company's Odessa list was to be
  brought in and sealed, darted if they wouldn't come. They carried the marker, which is why sleepers wake with powers.
- Mara and Ivo remember Bennet's men too, and the girl from Pod 14 who once visited the annex.

## Who gets it

- **New players.** Everyone who would wake in the bunker lives the Last Day first.
- **Skipped:** players who have already played two minutes or have an awakened power. They are told where the bunker
  is, as before.
- **Waiting for the city.** On a new world, Odessa is built the first time it's needed. The first player waits on a
  dark screen showing build progress, and the city builds three times as fast while anyone waits.
- **The edge of town.** Odessa doesn't end in fog: past its last street lie suburbs (ranch houses, a gas station, a
  motel, a diner, a strip mall), then oil country (pumpjacks, tank batteries, a water tower, a freight train by a grain
  elevator) and open prairie along highways to the horizon. The outskirts go up after the city, so nobody waits on them.
- **One day at a time.** The prologue and the Space-Time vision share the city and take turns.
- **Joining friends.** Anyone who joins while a group is still in its apartments, or out in the evening with at least 40
  seconds of it left, joins that group's day: a late arrival wakes in an apartment with the door already open. Anyone
  who joins later than that waits on a dark screen until the day ends, then lives the next one.
- **Groups of any size.** Apartments are shared past twelve; the vault waits for everyone; Noah's guards stun everyone
  in the hall (Noah himself if you're alone), and everyone stays dark until the pods, however many there are.
- **Leaving.** Logging out mid-day starts you over next time, unless you were already sealed in your pod. Then you
  simply wake in the bunker.

## Settings and commands

Common config, under `[bunker]`:

| Key | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prologue` | `true` | New players live the Last Day before waking. Off, they wake in the bunker at once. Needs `enabled`. |
| `prologueReadySeconds` | 120 | How long the ready players wait in the lobby before they're offered **[Go without them]**. |
| `prologueEveningSeconds` | 180 | How long the ordinary evening lasts before the light. |
| `prologueRunSeconds` | 150 | How long players have from the light to reach the safe house before the agents dart them (never less than about 129 seconds: the fight, the wave and 70 seconds after it). |

Commands:

- `/lastday ready`: say you're ready, in the lobby (the chat button runs it; typing *ready* in chat does too).
- `/lastday go`: once the ready ones have waited two minutes, go out without whoever hasn't readied (the chat button runs it).
- `/heroesevolved prologue replay <players>`: live the day again from the apartment.
- `/heroesevolved prologue skip <players>`: wake them in the bunker now.
- `/heroesevolved prologue jump <evening|light|safe|noah|pods> [ticks]`: for previews.
- The admin menu's **To cryopod** (Player tab, Admin row): the day jumps to everyone sealed in their pods and plays on to the waking; pressed while the city is still being built, the day starts there.

## For developers

- **Code:**
  - `story/prologue/PrologueService`: the phases.
  - `PrologueTimeline`: the light's growth, Ted's waves, the big wave's front, the daze and the surges, shared with the client.
  - `LastDayFight`: Peter and Sylar's fight as one script (positions, poses, beats, the facades and the chunks), drawn by
    `client/effects/intro/LastDayArt`.
  - `LastDayRescue` (the people under the rubble), `LastDayWave` (the windows and the knock-down), `LastDayCrumble` (the
    buildings coming apart), `LastDayGuide` (the thread of light's routes, drawn by `client/effects/intro/GuideArt`),
    `LastDayDamage` (everything broken, put back), `LastDayShelter` (Odessa's people going down the stair, shut out by the
    vault, sealed in the spare pods).
  - `story/bunker/PodSleepers`: the people in the pods, in the past and the present (the present's are kept in step with
    who has a pod).
  - `client/effects/intro/WaveApproach`: the rumble before the wave (`client/sound/WaveRumble`) and the head turned to it.
  - `client/DazeView`: flat on your back, out cold for `PrologueTimeline.BLACKOUT` with the screams slowed and smeared
    (`PanicSounds.dazed`), blinking awake, ears ringing (shared with the radiation awakening, which has no blackout).
  - `client/sound/LastDayMusic`: the two stems; `tools/generate-lastday-music.py` makes them, the ringing and the crash.
  - `PrologueCommands`.
  - `story/actor/StoryActorEntity` and `entity/client/StoryActorRenderer`: Noah, Claire, Peter and Ted.
  - `time/intro/city/Apartments`: the Odessa Arms.
  - `story/bunker/PastBunker`: the bunker as it was, shared with Night Three.
- **Skins:** drawn by `java tools/GenerateStoryActorTextures.java`.
- **Where it is:** the safe house is built once under Primatech Paper in the city dimension, its roof below anything
  the city rebuilds. Its north door is at `CityGrid.SHAFT_X/Z`.
- **The way down:** blueprint `lastday_stairwell` (switchback and vault frame, placed at the bunker's origin by
  `PrologueService.LastDayData.reset`). The city draws the top step, the slot and the rails in `Landmarks.safeHouse`
  (`CityGrid.STAIR_*`). `PrologueService.vault` swings the vault door; `walkable` in `PrologueGameTests` walks the route.
- **Pod 14's glass:** the pod's `cracked` state, its `_cracked` models and `java tools/GenerateCryoCrackTexture.java`.
- **Tests:** `PrologueGameTests`.
- **Photographs:** `./gradlew runClient --init-script tools/prologue-preview.init.gradle` writes every beat to
  `build/prologue-preview/screenshots/`.
