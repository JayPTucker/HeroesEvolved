# The Last Day

Every new player's story starts on the Last Day in Odessa, 103 years before they wake in the bunker under spawn. You
live the evening the city ended. You wake in your own apartment, walk its streets and run when the light appears.
Noah Bennet seals you in a pod "for your own good". Then the [usual waking](the-past.md#the-first-five-minutes) begins,
in the pod with the same number.

## What happens

1. **Your apartment.** You wake in the **Odessa Arms**, a brick walk-up a block west of Primatech Paper. The caption
   reads *Odessa · 6:02 PM*, and your name is on the plate by your door. There are twelve apartments on three floors,
   each with a bed under the window, a kitchen, a table and a radio. With more than twelve new players at once, they
   share: the emptiest apartment takes the next person, and the plate lists who lives there.
   - **Waiting for each other.** The door stays shut until everyone who woke with you is ready. Try your door, or click
     **[I'm ready]** in chat. If someone never readies, the doors open anyway 60 seconds after the last person arrived.
2. **The evening.** The doors open on an ordinary evening, three minutes long.
   - Odessa's people chat on the sidewalks, look in shop windows and walk round the block. It's unhurried: a
     conversation every thirteen seconds or so near you, with a breath between lines.
   - Their talk has hints of what's coming: Ted Sprague looked sick today, Isaac Mendez painted the street on fire, and
     there are guards on every door at Primatech.
   - Two Company agents stand outside Primatech's lobby.
3. **The light.** Far up the avenue, a man begins to glow.
   - For fifteen seconds nobody runs: people stop and stare at it, and wonder aloud.
   - Far up the avenue, over the light, two small figures meet in a flash: Peter Petrelli and Sylar, fighting in the sky.
     Pale condensation trails behind Peter and dark smoke behind Sylar; their clashes flash and boom (the sound arrives a
     moment late, from that far away). About a minute in, Sylar is hurled into the tower beside the plaza in a burst of
     dust, and Peter dives into the light. You see it all up close later, if a friend's power takes you back.
   - Then the sirens start, the crowd runs south toward Primatech, and you're told where to go.
   - Two columns of light mark the way: one over Primatech's doors, one at the head of the stair inside.
   - The agents call people in (three calls, twenty seconds apart): *"Down the stairs in the lobby! Go!"* Just inside the doors, a railed slot in the lobby
     floor opens on a **wide stair** down. It goes down east, turns at a landing, and comes back west to a **vault
     door** under the lobby. The vault was shut all evening (you can find it if you explore); it swings open when the
     light appears.
   - You have **two minutes** from the light. It grows the whole time and surges eight times, far apart at first and
     closer and closer toward the end. Each surge whites out the sky and shakes the ground a little harder, but you
     never see it go off.
4. **Too slow.** Anyone still outside when time runs out is darted by an agent behind them. They see one last, biggest
   surge, then nothing, and are carried down and sealed in a pod. Nobody dies and nobody holds the group up.
5. **The safe house.** Past the turn in the stair you're safe, and there's no going back up. The vault leads into the
   stasis bunker as it was that evening: lights on, nothing broken, every pod open and waiting, and Company guards at
   the vault and along the corridor. Once everyone is through (or after ten seconds), the vault slams shut behind them. In the stasis hall, Claire Bennet is begging her
   father:
   > Dad, I don't want to go, please! I can stop this!

   When everyone is in the hall (the scene waits up to 30 seconds for stragglers in the corridor), Noah walks her to
   **Pod 14**, shoves her in and seals it. He promises to come back for her. Then he turns to you:
   > I know you don't understand. This is for your own good.

   He raises a Company taser and fires at each of you in turn.
6. **The pods.** You come to sealed in your own pod, labelled with your name.
   - Screaming far above, and the ground shaking harder and harder.
   - Claire, through the glass of Pod 14: *"Uncle Peter... please..."*
   - One terrible blow through the rock. Every light in the hall dies, then stutters back (about a third stay dark), and
     stone breaks from the ceiling and comes down the aisle in a haze of dust. Then silence.
   - The pod hums on. Frost creeps across your sight, and everything goes black.
   - **103 years later...**
7. **Waking.** The bunker under spawn wakes you in the pod with the same number (*STASIS FAILURE*, the alarm, and the
   pod counter reading 37,620 days), and the story carries on as [The Last Signal](the-past.md).

Everything the story says goes to chat, one line per beat, and nothing is laid over the scene:
- **Where you are:** a caption line with a grey subtitle (*Odessa · 6:02 PM*, *Something is wrong downtown.*).
- **What to do:** a line starting », written once when it changes.
- **Who speaks:** Noah, Claire and the agents as `<Name> line` (no floating text).
- **What happens to you:** one line each for the dart, the stun gun and the pod.

The only text on screen is the vanilla title card *103 years later...*, and the *PAST* label in the corner, which hides
while the screen is black. Odessa's people still talk in small speech bubbles in the street: their chatter is the city,
not the story.

### Claire and Peter

Peter Petrelli, her uncle, is the one up there with Ted, and Claire says so: in the safe house while the others come down
(*"I can help Peter. He'll die if I'm not there!"*, *"He's my uncle, Dad."*), when the vault shuts, all through her
father's scene, and once more through the glass of her pod. Each line is said once.

### Noah's glasses

Noah wears a real pair of **Horn-Rimmed Glasses**, an item drawn on his face, not painted on his skin. Players can wear
them too (use them, or put them in the head slot): a spare pair is in the security chest of a newly built bunker, and
they can be crafted from two sticks, two glass panes and an iron nugget (sticks in the top corners, panes either side of
the nugget below).

## In the present

- **Pod 14 is open and empty.** Its plate reads *SUBJECT 14 / C. BENNET / RELEASED*, and nobody is ever assigned to
  it. Frost still clouds its glass, clawed through from the inside, with a star of cracks where she struck it.
  **Claire's note** waits on a lectern beside it (put back if someone takes it): she woke decades early, healed
  through the cold, sat with the sleepers she couldn't safely wake, is sorry for what her father did, and went up to
  find out what was left.
- **Lockdown Order 7** in Records is Noah's order from that evening. Everyone on the Company's Odessa list was to be
  brought in and sealed, darted if they wouldn't come. They carried the marker, which is why sleepers wake with powers.
- Mara and Ivo remember Bennet's men too, and the girl from Pod 14 who once visited the annex.

## Who gets it

- **New players.** Everyone who would wake in the bunker lives the Last Day first.
- **Skipped:** players who have already played two minutes or have an awakened power. They are told where the bunker
  is, as before.
- **Waiting for the city.** On a new world, Odessa is built the first time it's needed. The first player waits on a
  dark screen showing build progress, and the city builds three times as fast while anyone waits.
- **One day at a time.** The prologue and the Space-Time vision share the city and take turns.
- **Joining friends.** Anyone who joins while a group is still in its apartments, or out in the evening with at least 40
  seconds of it left, joins that group's day: a late arrival wakes in an apartment with the door already open. Anyone
  who joins later than that waits on a dark screen until the day ends, then lives the next one.
- **Groups of any size.** Apartments are shared past twelve; the vault waits for everyone; Noah shoots each player in
  turn, and everyone stays dark until the pods, however many there are.
- **Leaving.** Logging out mid-day starts you over next time, unless you were already sealed in your pod. Then you
  simply wake in the bunker.

## Settings and commands

Common config, under `[bunker]`:

| Key | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prologue` | `true` | New players live the Last Day before waking. Off, they wake in the bunker at once. Needs `enabled`. |
| `prologueReadySeconds` | 60 | How long the apartments wait for everyone to be ready after the last arrival. |
| `prologueEveningSeconds` | 180 | How long the ordinary evening lasts before the light. |
| `prologueRunSeconds` | 120 | How long players have to reach the safe house before the agents dart them. |

Commands:

- `/lastday ready`: say you're ready (the chat button runs it).
- `/heroesevolved prologue replay <players>`: live the day again from the apartment.
- `/heroesevolved prologue skip <players>`: wake them in the bunker now.
- `/heroesevolved prologue jump <evening|light|safe|noah|pods> [ticks]`: for previews.

## For developers

- **Code:**
  - `story/prologue/PrologueService`: the phases.
  - `PrologueTimeline`: the light's growth and surges, shared with the client.
  - `PrologueCommands`.
  - `story/actor/StoryActorEntity` and `entity/client/StoryActorRenderer`: Noah, Claire, Peter and Ted.
  - `time/intro/city/Apartments`: the Odessa Arms.
  - `story/bunker/PastBunker`: the bunker as it was, shared with Night Three.
- **Skins:** drawn by `java tools/GenerateStoryActorTextures.java`.
- **Where it is:** the safe house is built once under Primatech Paper in the city dimension, its roof below anything
  the city rebuilds. Its north door is at `CityGrid.SHAFT_X/Z`.
- **The way down:** blueprint `lastday_stairwell` (switchback and vault frame, placed at the bunker's origin by
  `PrologueService.LastDayData.reset`). The city draws the top step, the slot and the rails in `Landmarks.safeHouse`
  (`CityGrid.STAIR_*`). `PrologueService.vault` swings the vault door; `walkable` in `PrologueGameTests` walks the route.
- **Pod 14's glass:** the pod's `cracked` state, its `_cracked` models and `java tools/GenerateCryoCrackTexture.java`.
- **Tests:** `PrologueGameTests`.
- **Photographs:** `./gradlew runClient --init-script tools/prologue-preview.init.gradle` writes every beat to
  `build/prologue-preview/screenshots/`.
