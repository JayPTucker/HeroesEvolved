# The Wither: Containment Failure

**One idea: a broken cage around a hungry star.** Its heads, ribs, attacks and arena are all being drawn toward the
Nether Star inside its chest. Its palette is charred bone, cold mint light, pale white and the violet-black breach.

## Well, That's Not Normal...

Build the familiar four-block soul-sand or soul-soil body and place the three Wither skulls. The Wither takes shape
where you built it. Then its ribs unlock. Its chest opens into a bright, impossible depth; each head is pulled into
it, the body folds after them, and an unstable breach is left hanging at the summoning site. There is no spawning
explosion outside the arena. Nearby witnesses earn **Well, That's Not Normal...**

Walk into the breach to enter. You need an awakened ability at **Power Level 16 or higher**. A lone player can fight;
friends enter separately and can join late. Only one Wither encounter is open at a time; another ordinary summoning
while it is open returns its ingredients instead of creating an unsafe second Wither.

**The breach stays open throughout the encounter.** Its partner waits in an alcove at the arena's entrance. Retreat
through it whenever you need to. Damage from that shelter cannot hurt the boss. If everybody retreats, the Wither
recovers and the abandoned attempt resets; the breach remains available for another try. Health grows with additional
fighters and never shrinks during an attempt. A server restart returns fighters safely and leaves the entrance ready
for a fresh attempt. Death drops are kept for their owner and returned on respawn.

## Inside the cage

A circular blackstone floor **81 blocks across**, pale fossil ribs rising **48 blocks above it**, three glowing containment anchors and thick
cover pillars surround a fractured central dais. The floor's radial channels lead toward its star. The entrance has
a sheltered return alcove. It is a separate dimension, rebuilt before a new attempt; the summoning site's terrain
stays intact.

There are **46 blocks of usable flight height**. The center stays open for aerial turns; the ribs lean inward only
around the perimeter. Tall cover pillars work at several heights. The Wither rises toward flying opponents, and its
marked lanes, skulls and star discharge reach the air. Flying clears the floor pulse, but does not skip the fight.
Crossing the outer bounds returns you to the entrance ledge instead of dropping you into the void.

## Three phases

| Phase | Health | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Threefold Hunger** | 100-67% | A hovering, intact cage; marked skull rain, three sight lines, an expanding pulse and a tether. Its chest opens before its heavy attacks. |
| **Broken Vessel** | 67-33% | Its ribs spread, it fights lower, cracks light the dais, and skeletal flankers appear. Adds a committed lunge and a ground slam with a long recovery. |
| **Critical Mass** | 33-0% | Its chest stays open, fragments orbit the exposed star, the room's channels blaze, and its final score gains a doubled pulse. Adds an arena-wide star discharge: hide behind solid cover. |

| Move | Warning | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Threefold Verdict** | Three fixed mint lines assemble through the fighters before they fire | Step off the lines or put solid cover between you and its heads |
| **Hollow Rain** | An iris marks each fighter's position, then skulls descend toward those fixed spots | Move; redirect a skull with a suitable defensive power |
| **Starved Pulse** | A ring charges at its feet, then rolls outward | Jump the passing ring, take height, or use a defense |
| **Grave Tether** | A thread tightens between a head and a marked position | Break sight or leave its narrow path before the pull lands |
| **Ribbreaker** | A broad lane marks a committed lunge | Step aside; attack during its recovery |
| **Ossuary Slam** | The chest flares and a broad ring fills where it will land | Leave the ring, then punish its recovery |
| **Event Horizon** | A three-second chest charge sends rays outward, ending at real cover | Hide behind a pillar or interrupt the feeding anchor |

## Turn its hunger against it

One anchor lights while it feeds a heavy move. Its glass can be broken while it is lit; breaking inactive glass does
nothing. The active anchor backfires into the Wither, taking a tenth of its health through its normal damage caps and
leaving its core exposed. A skull striking the active anchor can do the same. No particular power is required.

A charged melee hit, critical arrow or heavy single hit can interrupt an ordinary windup. Heavy moves leave a spent
window with increased damage. Phase finishers force the next phase and earn a long stagger; in the final tenth they
can finish the Wither. Its warning positions are fixed before attacks land, and its telegraph shapes share the
server's geometry.

| Powers | Their place in the fight |

| Superspeed | Redirect skulls with Wind Arms; reach an active anchor quickly |

| Cryokinesis | Chill and slow; never freeze the boss solid |
| Telepathy, Empathic Mimicry | Their usual boss-strength effects and the same reachable anchor objectives as every other player |
| Regeneration, Power Amplification, Invisibility | Sustain, support and protection while a teammate counters; solo players can use the room's cover |
| Electrokinesis, Radiation, Technopathy, Vibe, Shapeshifting, Space-Time | Their normal kits remain useful under the boss rules and damage caps; control buys an opening rather than skipping the encounter |

## Rewards, returns and music

The first lethal blow begins a death scene: the cage tears apart, the star's light escapes in three streams and folds
shut. A Nether Star survives. Each participant receives a Fractured Star, and first-time winners receive containment
notes pointing toward the powered End portal. A Fractured Star can be crafted into a Breach Seed for another attempt.
Later victories can also leave a record of the theme. Rewards are delivered safely before the arena resets.

The score has three original, synthesized versions of one theme: a restrained three-part heartbeat, heavy low drums
and strings for the broken cage, and an urgent final pulse around a distorted choir. Phase changes move to the next
bar with a short stinger; its death ends on a last chord. It follows the Music slider and is heard inside the arena.
The Gemini: Heroes Reborn library is a reference for structure, texture and dynamics; none of its recordings or
melodies is included in the mod.
