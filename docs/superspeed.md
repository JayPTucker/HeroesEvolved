# Superspeed

A new unique ability set (`heroesevolved:superspeed`) combining the movement and reaction theme of [Heroes' Enhanced Speed](https://heroes.fandom.com/wiki/Enhanced_Speed) with Flash-inspired golden lightning, rapid strikes, and a running whirlwind. Enhanced Speed informs the contact rescue, brief wall traversal, and resistance to slowed time while already moving. This is a bounded Minecraft adaptation, not literal intercontinental or faster-than-light travel.

## Reworked kit (Daphne Millbrook and the Flash)

Daphne Millbrook said the world stops when she runs; she was also a thief who could be gone and back
before you blinked. The kit keeps the Flash's golden lightning and adds her blink.

### Momentum

Running builds **Momentum** (0 to 100), shown as a gold bar under your ability keys, with notches at
30, 50 and 60.

| Momentum | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 30 | Blitz can spend 30 for harder blows |
| 50 | Lightning crackles over your body as you run |
| 60 | You **buffet** enemies you run past: 2 damage, thrown aside, once a second each |
| 80 | You **vibrate through walls**: run into a wall up to 3 blocks thick and you come out the far side, still running |
| 100 | You **break the sound barrier**: a boom, and a cone of white rings behind you |

**Vibrate** (at 80 Momentum) shakes your molecules through whatever you run into. Hit a wall up to
3 blocks thick and you pass straight through it: gold ripples on both faces, a blur of you through
the stone, and your Momentum and heading carry on. Crouch to stop it. Bedrock, barriers and
protected ground still stop you, and you never end up inside a block. **Blitz** vibrates too: at 80
Momentum it reaches an enemy you can see through a window or gap by passing through the wall around it.

**Sudden stops** throw a gust. Come down from a run of at least 18 blocks a second (with 40 or more
Momentum) to a third of that pace in a tick or two, by letting go of the keys or by running into
something, and the air you were dragging keeps going. It rushes on past you in pale streaks and
spreads, a bow wave of pressed air rolls ahead, skid dust billows forward off your feet, grit is
flung on, and two scorched skid marks lie behind you, glowing for a moment. Anything within 4.5
blocks is shoved back a couple of blocks, hardest straight ahead; it does no damage. Players are
only shoved where PvP allows it. Bursts (Blitz, Lightspeed, dashes) do not count as stops.

At 50 or more, your next real punch is still the charged strike. Ability hits never spend it.
Lightspeed spends all your Momentum.


### Hands at speed (while Accelerate is on)

- **Impact punches.** A real punch thrown while running lands with your actual speed behind it: from
  about 12 blocks a second up to full force at 40. That's up to 10 bonus damage against mobs (2.5
  against players), plus a shockwave, a camera jolt, and the target blasted along your running line. A
  mob that hits a wall while flying takes up to 6 more. Each target can take one impact every
  half-second, and every impact costs you 15 Momentum. This stacks with the 50-Momentum charged strike.
- **Rapid punches.** Your weapon recharges three times faster. Hold attack on an enemy to punch every
  few ticks without waiting out its flinch. Every blow after the first in a flurry does half damage
  (a third against players).
- **Catch.** Right-click an arrow or thrown item in flight within 7 blocks to catch it out of the air
  for 2 stamina. Arrows that could be picked up and thrown items go into your inventory. Fireballs
  and skulls are snuffed out. Perception also lets you catch shots it slows, including TACZ bullets,
  while Accelerate is off. Slowed bullets glow and return matching ammo when caught.
- **Perception** slows everything within 12 blocks (16 with Long Perception) to a third of its speed,
  or a sixth with Flash Time. Other players move and recharge attacks more slowly across the server.
  Projectiles keep moving even with Flash Time. Perception has gold clock rings, hanging sparks and a slow heartbeat.
- **Lightning Throw** now uses your own gold lightning.

### Cyclone and its tornado

Your body (and your camera) stays at the eye of the ring, but nobody sees you there. A stand-in with your
skin, armour, held items and cape runs laps of the ring instead, about one every three-quarters of a
second. It leans forward into the run and in toward the eye, with six fading afterimages strung
out behind it. In first person you watch yourself run circles round you, and your arms are hidden. A
gold trail with lightning jumping off it follows the runner. Pale bands of wind are dragged round
after it, and dust and grit circle the rim.

Keep the key held for 30 ticks and the ring **rises into a tornado**:

- **Its shape.** The funnel stands on a slim foot inside your ring, pinches to a neck a few blocks up, and
  flares to a lid of storm cloud 26 blocks up and 11 across. It is billowing dust and cloud that turns faster
  near the ground, with streaks spiralling up its wall, your gold lightning wound up inside it, and debris
  carried up it.
- **It draws things in.** Creatures and loose items out to **24 blocks** are dragged in along the ground,
  swirling, faster the closer they get.
- **It carries them up.** Whatever reaches the funnel is whirled round its wall and carried up it to about
  12 blocks, taking 2.5 + tier / 2 every half second, with no fall damage while it is carried. Shots are
  thrown out.
- **It leaves them no air.** Anything that breathes loses its breath inside it (a player sees their bubbles
  drain) and then drowns in the air every half second until it is let go.
- **Hold it to anchor it.** Holding it up costs 4 stamina a second. Keep holding for **three seconds after it
  rises** and it is anchored: a roll of thunder, and it costs nothing more. Let go then and you step out of it,
  free to move, while it stands on its own until **thirty seconds** after it rose. Let go any sooner (or run out
  of stamina) and it comes down at once.
- **Coming down.** It lifts off the ground and thins away from the bottom up, and everything it carried is
  thrown out of it from wherever it was, so the fall does the rest.
- **Your view.** While you are running your own tornado, the camera steps well back from it, about 26
  blocks behind it along the way you are looking and 12 up, and holds still, so you can see the whole funnel,
  what it has caught and your runner circling its foot. It returns to you when you step out or it comes down.

A quick press is still the three-second ring. The server's wind and the drawing share one shape
(`TornadoShape`) and one wind (`TornadoWind`), and Flight's Tempest uses the same tornado in the sky's colours.

### Running the heat out of a radioactive player

Press the Cyclone key (X, tier 4 and up) with a radioactive player within 10 blocks who is running **Hot**
(40+ Core) or about to blow, and instead of a Cyclone you run rings round *them*, about twice as fast as a
Cyclone, 4.5 blocks out (6.5 while holding a blast in, to keep clear of it). Your runner circles them inside a spinning wall of gold light, and their heat is dragged out on the wind:
orange streams spiral out of their body to the wall, with green motes pulled after them.

- **Cooling.** While they are only Hot, every tick of running carries a point of Core out of them, until they are
  cool (10 Core) or ten seconds pass. Let go of the key and you stop.
- **Containment.** If their containment is failing, or the blast is already charging (theirs or an involuntary
  meltdown), the run **holds it in**: neither the warning nor the charge runs down while you run. Hold it for **five seconds**, while it builds (the wall of light
  grows denser and taller, rings of pressure close in on them faster and faster, gold lightning leaps from the wall to
  them, the ground shakes harder and the wind climbs), and it is **defused** with a cold shock rolling out over the
  ground; their Core settles at 30. Once you are containing a blast you can't let go.
- **The cost.** The heat you carry is paid in stamina: 1 stamina a unit at tier 5, 1.8 below it. When your stamina
  runs out you overexert and the heat burns you instead (0.15 health a unit at tier 5, 0.4 below it). A full
  containment carries 120 units (1.2 a tick): a tier-5 runner with stamina to spare comes out unhurt; a tier-4 runner at full
  health and stamina barely lives; one already drained does not.
- **Burning away.** If the heat would kill you, it has beaten you, but you take the rest of it with you: **the
  blast stops at once**, and their Core drops to nothing. Your runner keeps running the ring as they burn: their body
  glows white-hot and cracks with gold light, then comes apart from the head down, each glowing cube left behind on
  the ring where it broke off, drifting up on the heat and cooling to ash. They slow as they go, and the last of them,
  their legs, crumbles where they come to a stop. Nothing can hurt you while you burn, and you die only when it is
  done, about five seconds after the heat beat you. The death message reads that you burned away holding back a
  meltdown.

### Overrun: outrunning the last minute

Speedsters at **tier 5 (Power Level 15)** can run so fast they overrun time. There is nothing to press.

1. **Run flat out.** With Accelerate on, sprint at 20 blocks a second or more with at least 90
   Momentum. A corner or a jump is forgiven (half a second); easing off for longer lets the charge fade.
2. **The wake** shows after about eight seconds flat out. Violet and gold streams braid along the
   path you just ran, a clock face turns backward at your feet, faster and faster, and glints are shed
   behind you. Near the end, rings in the air ahead rush at you.
3. **The breach** opens after twenty seconds. A ring of light appears in the air thirteen blocks ahead,
   with the clock's marks round it and its hands spinning backward. A tunnel of rings leads down into
   it and the air streams in. For three seconds it comes on toward you.
4. **Run through it** and you go back **a minute**. The world snaps back at once: everything within 64
   blocks of where you stood then, and of everywhere you have been since, is as it was. That covers blocks
   (placed or broken, even after you left the spot), chests, creatures, and people with what they carried.
   Things that turned up since are gone. **Deaths are undone**: yours, and anyone else's in reach. They come
   back alive as they were, and what their deaths dropped is gone again. Then everyone it reaches is
   **carried back through their own minute**: you first, gliding backward along the way you ran for three
   seconds under a violet edge, with the rewind's violet trail streaming along it. If you went through a
   portal in that minute, the trip goes back through it. Anyone else it reaches does the same along their
   own path. Then you all land as you were. See
   [Going back](space-time-manipulation.md#going-back) for the details, shared with Rewrite the Moment. Where you left, the breach
   slams shut in a flash and a ripple of time spreads out. Where you arrive, a column of light comes
   down and a ring of wind runs out over the ground. Ripples of time spread and lightning crosses the
   ground. Light is drawn into you, and a clock face under your feet spins its hands backward and winds
   down to rest. The action bar reads *One minute ago.*

**On the HUD**, under Momentum, a line says where Overrun stands: *Overrun in 7:42* while it cools down (or
while it is still gathering its minute of history, after logging in or after the last one); *Overrun ready*
when it can charge, with a violet bar that fills as you run flat out; the bar pulses once the breach is open.
Below Power Level 15 the line only appears while you run flat out, to say it needs Power Level 15.

Ease off while the breach is open and it closes; the wake drops back to where it first showed. Overrun
needs a full minute of history (none after logging in). Dying or going through a portal ends the run,
but not the minute behind you, so you can still go back into it: past your own death, or back through the
portal. It works once every ten minutes. Your Mastery, cooldowns and powers are never rolled back.
It does not work in the Odessa vision.

The history is the one Time Manipulation's Rewind keeps (`TimeRewindBlocks`, `TimeRewindEntities`,
`TimeRewindContainers`, `TimeRewindPlayers`), sampled once a second and kept for 64 seconds instead of 16;
the trip back is `RewindTravel`. Like Rewind, it also rolls back other players within reach.

The old Quickstep lives on as Blitz with no target. Lightning Flurry became Blitz's flurry, and
Whirlwind became Cyclone. Redline is replaced by Lightspeed, and by Momentum itself, which now does
more on its own.

**Evolved trial:** run 20 km (20,000 blocks) with Accelerate.

Sprint movement uses native synced attributes. The starting movement multiplier ranges from 4x at level 1 to 8x at level 5. Running builds Momentum, adding up to another 2x, for a level-five peak of 10x. At least 50 Momentum empowers the next direct attack, consuming the charge for up to 12 bonus damage against mobs or 4 against players. The flurry deals 60% base damage against players. Other ordinary movement modifiers still apply. Camera expansion is capped at 1.12x to avoid extreme speed-induced distortion. While Accelerate is on you step straight up a full block (or a block and a slab) without jumping, sprinting or not. Run off a ledge of up to about two and a half blocks and you drop straight onto the lower ground, keeping your run, instead of hanging in the air; deeper drops fall normally. You take **no fall damage** while Accelerate is on. If you are on fire, half a second of running flat out (0.6 blocks a tick or more) fans the flames out, with a puff of smoke; it does nothing in lava.

At **level 3**, sprinting with Accelerate supports your feet on the water surface using matching client/server collision. This includes flowing water and water plants. Release sprint, crouch, or disable Accelerate to sink normally. It does not lift submerged swimmers, support lava running, or catch long falls. At **level 4**, sprinting into a wall provides up to thirty ticks of upward assistance; it resets after returning to clear ground. Steep falls are not caught. Movement still benefits from in-game feel testing across terrain and server latency.

Running with Accelerate costs only **part of vanilla's sprinting hunger**, less as you grow into the power:
60% at Power Level 1, falling evenly to 15% at Power Level 20 (vanilla charges by the block, and at fifty
blocks a second it would empty a full bar in about twelve seconds; Accelerate is paid for in stamina). At
Power Level 15, where Overrun opens, the twenty-odd seconds flat out it takes cost about nine food points,
under half a bar. Momentum only fades once you have really
stopped: a tick with no step while you are still running (moves arriving unevenly from the client) costs nothing.

While **Accelerate is enabled**, edible food is consumed **four times faster**, even when standing still. A normal 32-tick meal takes 8 ticks (0.4 seconds). Nutrition, item consumption, and food effects stay normal. Disabling Accelerate restores normal eating speed immediately, including during a meal. Bows, potions, milk, and other non-food uses are unaffected.

## Skill tree

Three branches, nine nodes, using the existing points, mastery bonuses and loadout controls:

| Branch | Optional skill | Behavior | Base cost / cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Efficient Stride / Control | Reflex Focus | Six seconds of reactive projectile dodging. Requires a clear sideways path and 3 additional stamina per dodge; at most one dodge each second. Does not block melee, explosions, falls or arbitrary damage. | 20 / 8 seconds |
| Kinetic Force / Force | Momentum Strike | Rush a visible target up to 40 blocks away through a clear path, ending within striking distance and dealing 6 + level damage. | 28 / 9 seconds |
| Quick Recovery / Survival | Slipstream Rescue | Carry one nearby villager, friendly creature, teammate, or crouching player up to twelve blocks ahead. Preserves their offset and sweeps both bodies before moving either. | 24 / 6 seconds |

Learn and equip these with the inventory **Skills** button. Passive discounts apply to initial activation costs and cooldowns, not ongoing running or dodge costs. Running earns Mastery for actual stamina spent. Each running interval is paid upfront so toggling cannot avoid the drain; walking does not consume running stamina.

## Power interactions and awakening

- Only one player can own Superspeed, through the existing persistent ownership system. It participates in the normal assignment pool and admin menus.
- The awakening has compact captions, rising amber arcs, a wind-ring release, and a non-damaging push to nearby enemies. It records completion through the existing intro history and does not forcibly launch the player into terrain.
- An actively sprinting, moving speedster can move through Between Seconds and Still World. Standing still does not confer immunity. Selective Stasis remains a separate counter. Being frozen solid, suppression, death, riding, elytra flight, dimension changes, logout or power removal ends enhanced running.
- Sylar notices successful activations normally. Once he learns Superspeed, his copied-power attack can rush through a checked path and strike at close range.
- The one trip through time is Overrun, a minute back, for tier-5 runners only. Rewind, the time stop and the rest stay Time Manipulation's. No unrestricted phasing through protected buildings or bedrock.

## Safety and performance

Dash and rescue paths check body-sized boxes every quarter block, up to forty blocks per action. They reject unloaded chunks, obstructed space, lava, fire and world boundaries. Horizontal bursts preserve downward velocity and accumulated fall distance. Normal running uses ordinary Minecraft collisions.

Running trails send at most ten bounded segment descriptions per second per moving runner. Clients render amber ribbons, short lightning branches and pressure rings using the existing particle/detail budget. Afterimages capture the actual skin and running limb pose at fixed world coordinates, fading from 42% opacity over 18 ticks. Slim/wide arms and enabled skin layers are supported; armor, capes and held items are not duplicated. At most 48 ghosts are retained across the scene, with lower emission at minimal particles. No extra entities or afterimage network packets are created.

A whirlwind considers at most 32 affected entities per update. Transient movement modifiers and runtime combat state are removed on cleanup. Existing imported recordings remain unchanged; new speed cues use vanilla wind and sweep sounds.

Both client and server need the updated mod.

## Verification

Run `gradlew.bat runGameTestServer --init-script tools/rewind-gametest.init.gradle` for gameplay tests. `SpeedRunGameTests` covers the tornado (it rises only when held, carries what it caught up its funnel, costs stamina, and throws everything out when let go), a quick press staying a ring, the sudden-stop gust (it shoves only what is close, does no harm, and fires once per stop), Overrun charging only at tier 5 and closing when you ease off, and crossing (position, a broken block, inventory and a newcomer all rolled back, then the cooldown), a block placed where you stood after you left, a death along your way and your own death undone, running hunger falling with Power Level, the ledge rules (a full-block step, a snap down a two-block drop but not a real fall, and no fall damage), running putting out flames (not a jog, not before half a second), and Wind Arms (blowing away what is ahead and nothing behind, harmlessly, putting out flames; lifting when pointed down). `gradlew.bat runClient --init-script tools/windarms-preview.init.gradle` photographs Wind Arms into `build/windarms-preview/screenshots/`. `RewindTravelGameTests` checks the trip back across worlds and its speed limit; `tools/rewind-travel-preview.init.gradle` photographs a Rewrite the Moment that goes back through the Nether. `gradlew.bat runClient --init-script tools/speedrun-preview.init.gradle` photographs the runner, the tornado, a real stop and a real twenty-second run into Overrun, into `build/speedrun-preview/screenshots/`. Superspeed coverage also includes native stride changes and cleanup, wall-safe bursts, preserved falling momentum, energy exhaustion and rapid-toggle protection, stopped-time/cold interactions, paid projectile dodges, contact rescue, momentum strikes, interrupted flurries and unique ownership.

`tools/verify-effect-geometry.ps1` checks the shared geometry and effect payloads; `python tools/verify-skill-content.py` checks all eleven trees, 99 nodes and 33 optional actions. `gradlew.bat build` produces the installable JAR. These checks do not replace a multiplayer visual/movement playtest.
