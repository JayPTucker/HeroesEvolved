# Ability effects

The mod has nine powers. `ModAbilities` registers them, each ability maps its
actions to five slots, and `AbilityUseService` validates and activates the assigned
action on the server. Persistent mechanics live in the flight, time, quake,
electro, telekinesis, and regeneration services.

## Visual system

`AbilityEffects` sends a small `AbilityEffectPayload` to nearby players in the
same dimension. It sends a single description instead of a packet for every
point along a ring or bolt. Long bolts select viewers by distance to the segment,
so a viewer at either end can see the effect. Tremor Sense scans go only to their
owner.

`AbilityEffectRenderer` builds the effect on the client using `PowerParticle`:

| Power | Visual language |
| --- | --- |
| Quake | Pale cyan pressure fronts, delayed concentric beam rings, ground impact waves, and descending plunge pulses. Existing terrain debris and camera shake remain. |
| Electrokinesis | Blue continuous jagged ribbons with white cores and short branches, plus radial discharge rings. |
| Telekinesis | Violet force rings, bands around held creatures, and orbiting motes. |
| Time manipulation | Lavender collapse/expansion rings, gold clock ticks, and rising afterimage motes. |
| Regeneration | Crimson and warm gold healing helices, moving blood threads, and a revival burst. |
| Flight | Body-aligned lingering vapor trails, curved sonic fronts, and layered cyclone wind ribbons. |

The three original, transparent sprites are generated by
`tools/generate-effect-textures.ps1`. To change the art, edit that script and run
it in PowerShell. Particle definitions and PNGs are committed assets; building
or playing does not require the generator.

Rings face their physical normal rather than the camera. Their scale and opacity
animate over their lifetime. Lightning uses connected camera-facing ribbons
rather than a dotted dust trail. The render bounds include entire ribbons and
expanding rings. Particles use the existing translucent particle atlas and world
depth testing; no shader mod or additional library is required.

Visuals do not apply damage, movement, cooldowns, or stamina changes. Quake slam
fronts follow the server's 0.8-block-per-tick wave, while the terrain-following
block dust and debris still come from the server. The flat light ring can be
occluded on uneven terrain; the existing block effects show the actual surface.
These are pressure-wave graphics, not screen-space refraction.

Reduced particle settings lower decoration density; Minimal keeps primary cues
and removes secondary motes/branches. The engine enforces a shared cap of 768
custom particles, and each effect has a bounded lifetime and geometry count.
The particle engine owns cleanup when a world unloads. The payload protocol is
now version 22, so clients and servers need the same mod build.

Flight and time sound registrations, audio files, volumes, pitches, and playback
calls are preserved. The new visual layer plays no sounds.

Flight pose and particle anchors share `FlightVisualGeometry`. The pressure rings
receive the torso pivot as their origin. Contrails start at the rotated feet,
including climbing/diving pitch and the cyclone's travel yaw, rather than a fixed
offset from the standing hitbox. Pitch is interpolated during rendering.

## Checking in game

Use a disposable creative world with commands enabled. Give yourself a power,
set level 5, and enable unlimited stamina using the existing commands:

```text
/heroesevolved power replace @s heroesevolved:quake
/heroesevolved power level @s 5
/heroesevolved stamina unlimited @s true
```

Default action keys are G, H, Z, X, and V. Shift plus a persistent action ends it;
Quake Blast ends when its key is released.

1. Quake: fire G horizontally and vertically; H on the ground and from the air;
   Z for a private expanding scan; hold X for the channel beam; V for earthquake pulses and its
   final aftershock. Check against nearby slopes as well as a flat surface.
2. Electrokinesis: replace the power with `heroesevolved:electrokinesis`; use
   chain lightning against several creatures, send Ball Lightning through open
   ground and into a wall, call Thunderstrike, and dash across open ground.
3. Telekinesis: replace with `heroesevolved:telekinesis`; grip and throw a
   creature, stop/release arrows, disarm an armed mob near loose items, and use
   the storm to lift multiple targets. Watch for tethers and landing impacts.
4. Regeneration: replace with `heroesevolved:regeneration`; heal while injured,
   share blood with a living target, activate Indestructible, and use Healing
   Aura with allies near both the caster and its eight-block boundary. The
   Resurrection slot reports readiness; a real revival triggers its particles.
5. Flight: replace with `heroesevolved:flight`; launch, boost, and use the
   cyclone. Dive Bomb from the air and check the boot trails and landing wave.
   Listen for the existing flight audio.
6. Time: replace with `heroesevolved:time_manipulation`; blink, slow, stop, and
   use snapshots. Move around corners before Rewind to check its ordered return
   trail; crossing dimensions should not connect unrelated world coordinates.
   Listen for the existing time audio.
7. Repeat with Decreased and Minimal particles, check another player's view,
   and rejoin a world to confirm transient effects clear.

Build with `./gradlew.bat build` using JDK 21. A successful build checks Java
compilation and resource packaging; it does not replace the visual/multiplayer
checks above.

## New action compositions

Each of the added actions has dedicated service cues rather than relying on a
generic activation ring. Existing damage, movement, stamina, cooldown, and sound
logic is preserved by these visual changes.

| Action | Activation, sustained motion, and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Quake Blast | Palm charge pulse; paired corkscrew filaments and pressure fronts during the channel; surface-normal contact rings against walls; a release pulse. |
| Ball Lightning | A moving luminous core with three rotating shell arcs and a wake; existing bolts lash nearby targets; an expanding three-dimensional discharge at impact. |
| Telekinetic Disarm | Curved violet filaments to the selected target and up to eight loose items, with particles flowing toward the caster. |
| Bullet Stop and Storm | Small contact rings when projectiles are caught/released; orbiting bands around held creatures; violet impact shards when thrown creatures hit a wall or land. |
| Healing Aura | Expanding crimson pulse, a gold boundary at the actual eight-block radius, counter-rotating arcs, rising motes, and the existing healing connections to allies. |
| Rewind | Restores nearby changed blocks, the player's position from 15 seconds ago, and lost health. Ordered lavender/gold ribbons unwind the path, with collapsing markers and reverse-spinning arcs. Requires a full history in the current dimension. |
| Dive Bomb | Boot-anchored twin vapor ribbons, a rotating wake, and layered ground rings with radial shards at landing. |

Curved ribbons animate each frame in `PowerParticle`, reuse the energy-streak
sprite, and contain twelve segments each. Moving orb shells follow projectile
velocity between two-tick updates. Primary cues remain on Minimal particles;
secondary shells, filaments, shards, and motes are reduced or omitted. All shapes
still share the 768-particle limit and have short lifetimes. No new sound playback
is introduced by the effect compositions.

## Rewinding blocks

`LevelBlockRewindMixin` records successful server block writes while a time user
is recording. This covers mining, explosions, fire, fluid changes, placement,
commands, and power damage using the normal world block API. Every player sample
stores a block-history revision, so edits in the same tick are ordered correctly.
Repeated edits to one position restore its earliest state after that revision.

The journal follows the player, recording changes within 32 blocks of their
position when each change happens. It retains sixteen seconds of history and at
most 16,384 changes per player. Exceeding that bound makes older rewind targets
unavailable until a complete recent history exists; it does not silently restore
only part of a target. Logout, death/respawn, dimension changes, and server shutdown
clear recording state.

Restoration includes saved block-entity data for changed containers. It removes
recorded drops that are still loose in the world and suppresses new drops during
restoration. It does not roll back player inventories or recover items that have
already been picked up, crafted, or transferred elsewhere. Container inventory
changes alone are not recorded unless the block state also changes. Changes made
before this build started recording cannot be reconstructed.

Destination collision checks use the planned restored blocks. All blocks are
restored before neighbor notifications, and restoration writes are recorded for
other time users so their overlapping timelines remain consistent.

Run the isolated server tests with JDK 21:

```text
./gradlew.bat runGameTestServer --init-script tools/rewind-gametest.init.gradle
```

The test sources and structure live in `src/gametest/` and are loaded only by that
verification run. Its world is under `build/rewind-gametest-world`; test classes
and test structures are excluded from the released mod.


## October 2026 effects redesign

All nine ability families use the updated visual system. Imported audio files,
sound registrations and playback timing are preserved.

| Power | Updated appearance |
| --- | --- |
| Radiation | White-hot vent beam with an orange halo and turbulent eddies; broken condensation fronts at detonation; a rising fireball, overlapping smoke column, rolling mushroom crown and spreading dust skirt. |
| Flight | Entirely new twin boot vortices following the pitched body, broad soft wakes at speed, curved sonic-pressure fronts, layered cyclone wind ribbons and vapor-based dive impacts/awakening. |
| Quake | Broken widening compression arcs, sharp pressure fronts and delayed jagged ground fissure highlights. Highlights are cosmetic and may be occluded on uneven terrain. |
| Electrokinesis | Slimmer short-lived white-core bolts, branching radial discharges, moving orbital cores and discharge impacts. |
| Cryokinesis | Moving diamond-shaped ice fragments with bright facets, lance highlights, rime rings, dense wind-driven snow, larger cold domes, local blizzard fog and frozen shells. |
| Telekinesis | Oppositely rotating tilted binding arcs, curved force tethers and orbital fields. |
| Telepathy | Dedicated fine waveforms linking minds, small head halos and outward psychic crescents, distinct from telekinetic restraint bands. |
| Time | Gold clock hands/ticks with animated reverse arcs, unwinding return trails and continuous temporal strands. The city cinematic uses the new textured mushroom cloud too. |
| Regeneration | Continuous double healing strands, sparse golden cells, flowing blood connections and layered aura petals. |

Flight trail reconstruction uses the existing synchronized pose and local entity
motion. It respects the trail interval setting, stops at landing/death, resets
across teleports/world changes, and skips players beyond 128 blocks. The server
no longer broadcasts individual contrail/cyclone cloud particles. Radiation's
vent beam also sends one description instead of particles along every quarter
block. Rapid Mending's redundant server particle spiral is removed.

Combat particles share a 768-particle limit; lingering flight vapor has its own 512-particle group. Per-tick admission limits are 240/140/72
for All/Decreased/Minimal; secondary motes have a separate smaller allowance.
Distance reduces decoration and arc tessellation; primary silhouettes remain
on Minimal under normal load. Saturation deliberately drops excess visuals.
Ring orientation and arc bases are computed once per particle. Cloud rendering
uses at most 256 vertices per cloud, three detail levels, distance/frustum
culling, back-to-front sorting and a four-cloud limit. Terrain destruction keeps
its existing bounded work queue; blast damage timing is unchanged.

Verification: 53 server GameTests passed; mesh checks exercise 5,220 rendered
particle frames (including crystals, motion, delays, vertical axes and culling),
all 51 effect message kinds, and cloud growth/cooling/fading at three detail
levels. Audio file hashes were compared before packaging. The cloud silhouette
was inspected in a software geometry preview; this is not an in-game screenshot
or an FPS benchmark. Check first/third-person flight, multiplayer scenes and all
three particle settings in Minecraft before judging the final motion and balance.

Both server and clients need this build (network protocol 22).


Flight vapor now persists eight seconds (five on Minimal), holding its opacity
for the first 40% of its lifetime before fading smoothly and widening gently.
Existing trails remain in world space after landing; new emission stops. They
use a separate 512-particle group so they cannot fill the combat particle pool.
At heavy multiplayer saturation some segments can be dropped to respect that cap.
Cloud rendering uses the shader camera transform exactly once; the regression
suite simulates moving/turning the camera at near and far distances to verify it.


## Quake aerial propulsion

Both Shockwave (G) and held Quake Blast (X) can propel Quake in midair without
hitting terrain. Jump and aim opposite your intended travel: looking down lifts
you, looking sideways pushes you the other way, and looking up drives you down.
The initial recoil adds to jump/movement velocity rather than replacing it.
Holding X gives an initial launch when you leave the ground and ongoing thrust
along the opposite aim vector. Releasing X ends thrust; momentum continues.
Downward aim can also launch directly from the ground. Horizontal fire while
standing stays braced. Propelled landings keep Quake's existing cushioning.
Repeated thrust has a directional speed limit and retains perpendicular momentum.


## Mouse-wheel Flight speed

Flight speed is selected with wheel up/down while airborne in an active Flight
session. Ctrl/Sprint is no longer a Flight accelerator; vanilla sprint acceleration
is also suppressed for this session while airborne. Ground sprinting is unchanged.
Normal Flight is setting 1. Levels 2, 3, 4 and 5 each unlock one faster setting,
for five total settings at Level 5. Faster settings use 40%, 60%, 80% and 100%
of the configured cruiseSpeed maximum. Hold Forward to travel at the selected
setting; releasing Forward returns to upright movement while retaining selection.
Wheel-down brakes toward the lower speed; returning to setting 1 brakes into
normal flight. Landing, toggling Flight off and power removal reset selection.
Level reductions immediately clamp the selected speed. Cyclone keeps its own
movement and ignores wheel adjustments. Pending Launch keeps its existing ascent.

The hotbar consumes the wheel normally on the ground, outside Flight and in menus.
During Flight, fractional wheel input accumulates into notches; one notch requests
one setting, and the server clamps to unlocked limits. The HUD shows the current
setting; a speed change displays the selected setting and maximum for the level.
Only active Flight sends periodic forward input. Faster forward movement uses the
existing high-speed stamina cost, wind, sonic boom and vapor. Imported audio files
are unchanged. Protocol 22 synchronizes Flight enablement and speed as well as pose.

Four isolated server GameTests cover every level's cap, large/invalid wheel
requests, real deceleration, forward release, landing cleanup, level reductions,
and Cyclone isolation. The complete suite has 60 tests. Client mouse-wheel feel
and sprint suppression still require an in-game playtest.


Cryokinesis now has a visible 24-block awakening dome and a 20-block Blizzard
dome, with immediate area freezing and reversible exposed terrain frost.
See cryokinesis.md for thaw timing, immunity, terrain limits and verification.
