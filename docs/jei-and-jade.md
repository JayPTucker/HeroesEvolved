# JEI and Jade

Heroes Evolved works with **JEI** (Just Enough Items: the item list and recipe book) and **Jade** (the box at the top
of the screen that names what you are looking at). Neither is needed: install either, both or none.

## JEI

- **Every item and block** from the mod is in JEI's list, with its name and tooltip. Type `@heroesevolved` in JEI's
  search box to see only ours. The Eclipse Emblem (the creative tab's icon) is hidden, since it can't be had.
- **Recipes.** Crafting, smelting and blasting recipes show as usual. The power-themed guns, their ammo and the EMP
  grenade show under TaCZ's own workbench pages (the EMP grenade is at the **Ammo Assembly Table**). Three machines have pages of their own:

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Suit Fabricator** | The eight protective suit pieces and their materials. Materials go in any of the six slots in any order; lead ingots from other mods count too. |
| **Centrifuge** | A Regenerative Blood Sample and a redstone charge spin into an Isolated Catalyst in 20 seconds. |
| **Synthesis Station** | The three brews of the Formula: water and bone meal make Buffered Saline, saline and a Helixite Shard make Unstable Compound, and compound and the catalyst make the Formula. Glowstone fuels it. |

- **Click the arrow** in a Suit Fabricator, Centrifuge or Synthesis Station to open its page. On the Suit Fabricator
  and Centrifuge pages, JEI's **+** button moves the materials in from your inventory; at the fabricator you still
  pick the pattern yourself.
- **Information pages** (JEI's "i" page; press U on the item) say where to find what can't be crafted: Helixite and
  lead ore, blood samples, the Formula, Sylar's Watch, and where the EMP grenade is made.
- **Keys.** The mod's keys only work in the world and JEI's only in menus, so they never fire together: R opens the
  ability wheel in the world and shows a recipe in a menu, and O opens the admin menu while JEI keeps Ctrl+O. The
  Controls screen still shows keys that two mods share in red; that is only a warning.

## Jade

Jade shows what your eyes see, never what a power is hiding:

| You look at | Jade shows |
| --- | --- |
| A shapeshifter in another shape, or a creature wearing a Borrowed Face | The shape they wear: a cow is a cow, a borrowed person has that person's name |
| A Decoy | The body it was left as |
| A player or creature you can't see (Fade, Vanishing Touch) | Whatever is behind them |
| A speedster or time traveller moving faster than time | Them, standing where you still see them: at the spot they started from |
| A flying rock, a carry, a ghost block or quake debris | Nothing: these are effects, not things |

And a few things worth knowing:

| You look at | Jade adds |
| --- | --- |
| A gifted mob | Its power and rarity, in the rarity's colour, and "Unbound" once it has broken loose |
| A villager whose power is running away with him | **Melting down: get clear!** |
| The Ability Dampener | Whether the field is up, its radius and its upkeep in FE a tick, and its stored energy |
| A Centrifuge or Synthesis Station | What is inside, and how far the spin or brew has got |
| Ice from Cryokinesis | An ice icon |

Each of these can be switched off in Jade's settings, under Heroes Evolved.
