# Sylar: the Collection

Character sources: [Gabriel Gray](https://heroes.fandom.com/wiki/Gabriel_Gray),
[Intuitive Aptitude](https://heroes.fandom.com/wiki/Intuitive_Aptitude) and
[Telekinesis](https://heroes.fandom.com/wiki/Telekinesis). This adapts the series' antagonist: a watchmaker who sees how
things work, takes powers apart to learn them, and keeps what he takes. His hall, his cases, his lines, the fight and
every number here are our own.

**What he wants from you:** to see how your power works, and to keep it.

**The one idea every attack speaks in: he sees how it works.** Every move is drawn first as a watchmaker's schematic in
brass: parts float apart and slide together, and the move lands as the last part seats. What you see is exactly when and
where it lands.

Sylar catches incoming TaCZ rounds in a telekinetic grip just before they reach him. The bullets hang for a little over half a second, then fly back at their shooter. Solid cover blocks the catch; staggering or suppressing him breaks his control and lets a held round continue on its original course.

## How you meet him: the prisoner and the list

Sylar doesn't stalk you. He *collects*, and for a hundred and three years the Company has kept him where he can't:
Level 5, under the bunker. That is how he is still alive. He keeps every power he has taken under glass, in a round
hall outside time he calls the Collection, and you meet him there in the end. First you meet him behind glass.

**He wakes.** The first time anyone's awakened power reaches Power Level 8 (`sylar.ticketPowerLevel`), they hear it:
*Somewhere under your feet, something has started to tick.* A hatch is now set in the floor of Cell 3 in the bunker's
containment block, Gray's old cell. (A world with no Sublevel 4 bunker gets the hatch in the ground beside world spawn,
and its coordinates.) Everyone else whose power gets that far later hears the same, and goes on his list.

**Level 5.** Use the hatch to take the lift down. Level 5 is its own place (`heroesevolved:level_five`), built once from
the `level_five` blueprint: a long corridor of empty cells (bars missing where something got out, a cracked stasis pod,
a skull), and at the far end his cell, behind nine panes of glass in a blackstone frame. The lift's button takes you
back up to the hatch. Nothing down here can be broken or built on, except in creative.

**The prisoner.** He stands in his cell, polite and helpful, and nothing hurts him through the glass. Stand near it and
he talks, a line every seven seconds while you stay:

| Visit | What he says |
| --- | --- |
| First | Who he is (Gabriel Gray, who fixed watches), and **something true about your power**: a real tip from its own page, such as Quake's "crouch between quakes" or Vibe's free aborted breach. Every power has one; no power gets "nothing in you yet" |
| Later | A line of small talk, then the lever: two keys, the sign says, and the keys rusted away long ago |
| Any | Each name crossed out since you last heard him, with its power, and what it means |

**The list.** On the wall by his cell, glowing, the Company's watch list of evolved persons:

| Down the left, then the right | How it is written |
| --- | --- |
| Nine old names (R. Calder ... T. Sprague, C. Bennet) | Crossed out in faded ink: their powers stand in his cases. Claire Bennet's and Ted Sprague's stand uncrossed in a world that kept them from him in the past |
| Three living names, in a careful hand | Gifted people in real villages nearby (a villager there takes the name, and a dormant gift, the first time someone comes near). Crossed out in fresh red ink as he takes them |
| The players he has listed | In yellow, at the bottom |
| Gabriel Gray | Crossed out last, once he is beaten |

The journal's Sylar page shows the same list, with each living name's power and roughly where their village is.

**Each night, one name.** When night falls, the first living name nobody is guarding is crossed out, and that villager
is emptied: their gift is gone, their name turns grey, and they tell you a man came to fix their clock. **Stand within
24 blocks of a listed villager when the night turns** and he passes them by (you hear something tick in the dark and
move away); he takes the next unguarded name instead. A night slept through still counts. Down in Level 5 he names
each one to you, and he was behind his glass the whole time.

**Your name is next.** The night after the last living name goes, his cell stands open. He comes for someone on his list:
behind them, wherever they are. *Behind you, a watch is ticking.* A few words, then his hand closes on their wrist and
they, and everyone within 16 blocks, are in the Collection. Go down to Level 5 while his cell is open and he is behind
you a few seconds after you step out of the lift.

**Or let him out.** The release lever in the guard post opposite his cell is stiff with rust: pull it twice. His glass
falls out of its frame, he thanks you, and the fight begins at once. Anyone still on the list is spared.

**After.** If the fight is lost, he comes again the next night. Beat him and the story is over: the people he emptied
get their gifts back, and his name is crossed off the list. From then on the fight comes back only with a **Repair
Ticket**, crafted from a Brass Movement he leaves (below): hold use on it for two seconds while the ticking gets louder,
and you and everyone within 16 blocks are folded into the Collection. **One fight at a time** on a server.

## The Collection

A round hall in a pocket dimension (`heroesevolved:the_collection`), rebuilt from the `the_collection` blueprint before
every fight, so whatever the last fight tore up, froze or broke is whole again.

- **The floor is a dial plate** 43 blocks across: a brass boss and a white dial on the dais in the middle, brass hour
  marks, a chapter ring, and a dark oak walk round the edge.
- **Twelve display cases** stand at the hours, 16 blocks out: a blackstone plinth lit from below, brass posts, a glass
  bell, a brass cap. Nine hold a power, each floating under the glass in its own colour. Three (three, seven and eleven
  o'clock) are kept empty, for yours.
- **The walls** are deepslate with dark oak wainscot, blackstone pilasters on the half hours, and a lit brass-framed
  panel behind every case. **The dome** has twelve brass ribs, like a dial overhead.
- **The past is in the cases.** Claire Bennet's case (twelve o'clock) holds her gift only if he reached Pod 14 on
  [Night Three](the-past.md); keep the pod shut and it stands dark. Ted Sprague's case (six o'clock) holds the light
  only if he saw it on the avenue at 6:14; keep him from it and it stands dark.

| Hour | Case | His move from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | Regeneration (Claire's), if he reached Pod 14 | Claire's gift: he gets back up once |
| 1 | Electrokinesis | Arc Lattice |
| 2 | Cryokinesis | Icefall |
| 3, 7, 11 | empty | your power, if he takes it: Your Own |
| 4 | Pyrokinesis | The Second Hand |
| 5 | Quake | Fault Line |
| 6 | Radiation (Sprague's), if he saw the light | The Light at 6:14 |
| 8 | Sonic Resonance | Resonance |
| 9 | Superspeed | Blur |
| 10 | Space-Time | Every Hour |

## The rule of the room: break the case he draws from

Every move except his own telekinesis draws on one of his cases. While he winds it up, **a thread runs from that case to
his hand and its glass is edged in gilt**. That case is open:

- **Break its glass** (a punch does it) and the power backfires into him: a tenth of his health, through every cap, and
  he reels for three seconds. That power is gone for the rest of the fight. Take his collection apart and his move list
  shrinks.
- **A case he isn't drawing from is locked.** Break its glass and it is whole again at once: time doesn't touch his
  collection.
- **What he takes from you goes into an empty case,** and that case stays open. Break it and your power comes home.
- Telepaths who read his mind mid-fight hear which case he is reaching for.

## Moves

Every move is read before it lands: his pose, a brass schematic in the world, and a sound. While he winds a move up his
loupe glints at his right eye, he stands still and throws no punches. Every spot is fixed when the windup starts, so
moving beats it.

| Move | Phase | Telegraph | What it does | How to answer it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hurl** (his own) | all | 1.2 s: he tears a slab out of the floor beside him and lifts it over his shoulder; a brass ring assembles where you stood | The slab lands: 8 damage within 2.5 blocks and you are thrown. It breaks up into a low mound of rubble (never on anyone), which is cover later | Leave the ring |
| **Reel In** (his own) | all | 0.6 s: a violet hook closes round you | Pulled down in front of him: 4 damage, slowed | Don't climb out of reach, keep far off, or hide from him for long |
| **Arc Lattice** (1 o'clock) | all | 1.2 s: brass lines assemble between pairs of cases, one through each of you (two lines, three from phase two) | Lightning along each line: 7 damage within 1.5 blocks, slowed | Step off the lines |
| **Icefall** (2 o'clock) | all | 1.4 s: a brass ring assembles under each of you | Ice bursts up: 6 damage, slowed and frozen. The floor stays blue ice (slick) for the fight | Leave the ring, and mind the ice after |
| **Fault Line** (5 o'clock) | all | 1.0 s: a crack assembles from his feet through you to the wall | The floor heaves along it: 7 damage and you are thrown up. The crack stays | Step sideways |
| **Resonance** (8 o'clock) | all | 1.0 s: brass arcs assemble round his feet | A low ring of sound rolls out to the wall: 6 damage and thrown back. It climbs the walls: anyone three or more blocks up is hit too | Jump it |
| **The Second Hand** (4 o'clock) | from phase two | 1.5 s: a brass hand assembles from the dais to the wall, a dashed arc shows which way it will turn | A hand of fire sweeps a quarter turn in two seconds: 6 damage and burning | Jump it, outrun it, or stand on the dais |
| **Blur** (9 o'clock) | from phase two | 1.2 s: three dashed lines assemble, each through one of you | He runs all three: 6 damage to anyone on a line | Step off the lines |
| **Appraisal** (his signature) | from phase two | 0.8 s: a ring under you, a lens over it | Still on the ring: you hang in the air for three seconds while a schematic of your power assembles round you. Then 6 damage, and **your power goes into an empty case** | Get off the ring. Held: sneak again and again, or have a friend land a charged hit on him |
| **Your Own** (a stolen case) | after Appraisal | 0.8 s: a line assembles from his hand toward you, in your power's colour | A bolt of your own power: 8 damage on the line. "Recognise it? Your power. My hands." | Step off the line; break the case |
| **Every Hour** (10 o'clock) | the Masterwork | 3 s: ten hours of the dial assemble in violet; **two opposite hours blaze gold** | Stopped time over the ten: 8 damage and held still for two seconds. He is spent after it | Stand in a gold hour |
| **The Light at 6:14** (6 o'clock) | the Masterwork | 3 s: he rises over the dais; brass rays assemble outward from him, stopping at the pillars, so the shadows show | Everyone he can see: 14 damage and burning. He is spent after it | Get behind a pillar (or a mound of his own rubble) |

Anyone who stands within three blocks of him is shoved back (5 damage). His moves aim at each fighter, so a party can't
hide behind one player.

**A world that kept him from the light.** If Sprague's case is dark, he still reaches for the light once in the last
phase: he rises, the rays start to assemble, and at seven tenths they come apart in his hands. Nothing lands, and he is
spent for six seconds. He doesn't try again.

## Three phases

| Phase | Health | Bar | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Viewing Hours** | 100–67% | purple, "Sylar · Viewing Hours · N cases" | He studies you: Hurl, Reel In, Arc Lattice, Icefall, Fault Line, Resonance. The first loop of his theme: a watch ticking, low strings, a music box |
| **Unlocked** | 67–33% | red | A shove throws everyone back. **The lights go out**; only the cases light the hall, and the hour marks glow red. **Echoes step out of his cases** (below). Adds the Second Hand, Blur, Appraisal and Your Own; he rests less between moves. The theme gains its drums, an ostinato and brass |
| **The Masterwork** | 33–0% | gold | He rises. **Four pillars rise from the floor** on the diagonals (their outlines warn you off their squares first). The great movement of a watch turns overhead. More echoes in a bigger party. Adds Every Hour and the Light. The theme doubles its pulse, and his motif moves to the brass and a choir |

Each phase change is a short transition (two seconds into Unlocked, three into the Masterwork) in which he can't be hurt,
marked by a stinger. The bar's name counts the cases still standing.

## Echoes

When his cases unlock, what is left of the people their powers were taken from steps out of them and fights for him:
**echoes**, gifted raiders each carrying one case's power (its moves, sigil and colour are a gifted mob's), named for
their hour ("Echo of Two O'Clock"). One answers the Unlocked phase, and one more for every two fighters past the
first; a bigger party meets more at the Masterwork. They never come from Claire's or Sprague's case, and never return
once gone.

**An echo's case stands open while it walks** (edged in gilt like the one he draws from). Break it and the echo fades
with it, and that power is gone from his collection for the fight, with no backfire, since he wasn't drawing on it.
So a case can be broken two ways: in a rush while he reaches for it (a backfire into him), or at your leisure while
its echo is out (and the echo goes too).

## Weak windows

- **Skill: break his concentration.** A charged hit while he winds a move up (a fully charged swing, a critical arrow,
  or any single hit of 8 or more) shatters the schematic: the move is gone and he reels for two and a half seconds,
  taking a quarter more from every hit. The same hit frees anyone he holds up in Appraisal.
- **Rhythm: he is spent.** After Every Hour or the Light he kneels for four seconds (six after a failed light) and takes
  half again from every hit.
- **Room: break the case.** The case he draws from backfires into him (above). Breaking the case that holds your power
  gives it back.
- **Power: switch him off.** An Ability Dampener round or field leaves him only his hands: no case moves until it wears
  off.
- **Finishers at his thresholds.** He glistens in the last stretch above each phase threshold (the last 6% of his
  health above two thirds and above one third) and in his last tenth. A finisher there plays in full: it takes 15% of
  his health through every cap, forces the next phase at once, and leaves him reeling for five seconds once the
  transition is over. In his last tenth it is the killing blow.

## What each power does to him

He is in the shared `c:bosses` tag, so every power's boss rules apply.

| Power | Against Sylar |
| --- | --- |
| Telepathy | Reading his mind mid-fight says which case he is reaching for. Drop It disarms him for a second |
| Empathic Mimicry | Copies one power in his collection (his telekinesis if nothing stands); Sylar's Mirror keeps all of them |
| Cryokinesis | Chills and slows, never freezes him solid |
| Density | Can't embed him |
| Carrying, Singularity, Rift Fall | He can't be lifted; he feels 15% of a pull; Rift Fall slams him where he stands |
| Power Amplification | Overcharge the Enemy stalls him for two seconds instead of sending his move wild |
| Finishers | Only at his thresholds (above): a finish forces his next phase, and in his last tenth kills him |
| Any area or block-breaking power | Breaks an open case as well as a fist does |
| Everything else | Works at full strength, under the caps below |

## Fight rules

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 320 with one fighter, half again for each more fighter who came with you (up to three times) |
| Per-hit cap | No hit takes more than 6% of his health (at least 10). A backfire, Claire's case and `/kill` are exempt |
| Per-second cap | After a first hit, no 20 ticks take more than 4% of his health |
| Range falloff | Full damage from attackers within 24 blocks, nothing from past 36 (the hall is 43 across) |
| Other attackers | Anything but a player (a golem, lava, a fall, a trap) does at most 3 a blow |
| Immunities | Falls, drowning, suffocation, being stuck in a wall |
| Idle healing | 5% of his health a second once nothing has hurt him for ten seconds and he can see nobody |
| Leash | Thrown out of the hall, he steps back onto the dais |
| Time | Ten minutes (`sylar.fightMinutes`); then he closes up |

**Your things are safe.** Whatever you drop dying in his hall is handed back to you when you respawn. A restart or a
logout mid-fight sends you home to where you stood.

## How it ends

| Outcome | What happens | A Repair Ticket |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Beaten** | His death scene, then his prizes land where he fell; the hall closes seven seconds later and everyone goes home. The first time, the story ends | Torn |
| **Everyone down** | "Ready for collection. All of you." The hall closes. In the story, he comes again the next night | Kept: read it again when you're ready |
| **Time up** | "Closing time." The hall closes | Kept |

Everyone goes home to where they stood when they were taken: wherever he found them, or Level 5.

He never runs from his own hall.

**Claire's gift.** If Claire's case is full when you land the killing blow, he goes down instead, and a thread from her
case starts mending him: break the case within five seconds and he stays down. Otherwise he gets up at a quarter of his
health, and her case is empty.

**His death.** Two to three seconds in which he kneels on the dais, everything he held streams out of him into the dark,
every case still standing shatters, the schematic that was always round him comes undone and falls, the lights come
back, and the music ends on his last chord. Then silence, and he falls.

## Rewards

- **The first time you beat him: Sylar's Watch** (its glass cracked, still ticking: use it to go back five seconds, to
  where you stood and how hurt you were; once a life) **and Gray's Ledger**, the catalogue of his collection. The ledger
  reads differently in a world that kept Claire's pod shut or kept him from the light, and its last line, in newer
  ink, points onward.
- **Every win: a Brass Movement** for each fighter, the works of his watch.
- **Beaten again:** a one in ten chance, for each fighter, of **the record of his theme**: *Gray & Sons - The
  Collection*, a music disc that plays the whole fight (eight bars of each phase, the stingers at the turns, his last
  chord) in any jukebox.
- **A re-fight:** craft a **Repair Ticket** from paper, a Brass Movement and an ink sac. A re-fight is the same hall,
  phases and rules, without the story's lines, and gives no second watch or ledger.
- Every prize lands where he fell; whatever is still lying there when the hall closes goes to its owner's pocket.

## Music

His theme is original synthesis (`tools/generate-sylar-music.py`), modelled on the reference library's gameplay
score: its pieces come in Exploration, Combat and Stealth states of one tune at rising intensity, and the theme rises
the same way, measured against Score 04's states and the Combat states of Scores 06 and 08 (their tempo, key, loudness,
brightness, drum density and how they build). Three loops of the same 24 bars in D minor at 68 bpm, one per phase,
each in three eight-bar sections with a crash and a swell at the turns:

| Loop | Phase | What plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viewing Hours | first | A watch's tick-tock, low strings on his own progression, a music-box motif, a far bell; distant drums and clockwork chimes come in and the loop builds, then falls back to its start |
| Unlocked | second | Taiko-style drums, toms, claps, hats and shaker, a spiccato string ostinato, clockwork anvils on the off-beats, brass swells and stabs |
| The Masterwork | third | All of it with the pulse doubled, his motif in the brass, a small choir, a high tremolo tightening toward the loop's end |

It plays as the game's music while you are in his hall (it follows your music slider). A stinger (a clock striking, a
drum, a brass rise and the choir) marks each phase change, and his death ends the music on a last chord (the watch's
last ticks, a bell, D minor settling to an open fifth), then silence.

## The voice

Everything he says goes through one paced queue per player, at least eight seconds apart. Warnings you must act on now
go to the action bar: the ring under your feet, being held, your power in a case, the gold hours, the light, Claire's
case. He has a line for meeting you, for each power you might have, for each phase, the backfire, a theft, Your Own,
Claire's gift, his defeat, everyone down and closing time.

## Configuration and commands

The common configuration's `sylar` section: `enabled`, `canStealPowers` (Appraisal still lifts you, but takes nothing),
`ticketPowerLevel` (default 8: the Power Level that wakes him and puts a player on his list) and `fightMinutes` (default
10). The old stalking keys are ignored.

```
/heroesevolved sylar next <player>     move the story on a step: wake him for them, cross out the next name, open his
                                       cell, or send him to them now
/heroesevolved sylar ticket <player>   a Repair Ticket in their pocket
/heroesevolved sylar fight <player>    open the Collection for them and everyone near them now
/heroesevolved sylar end               close the Collection; everyone goes home
/heroesevolved sylar reset             put him back to sleep and forget his list (a hatch already placed stays)
/heroesevolved sylar status            the story, the fight, his wins, and which of the past's cases stand full
```

The admin screen's World tab has Next, Ticket, Fight and End.

## Elsewhere in the story

Sylar is still the actor in the story's scenes: the Last Day's fight with Peter, the avenue at 6:14 (keep him from the
light) and Night Three (keep him from Pod 14). Each of those changes his Collection and his list as above. He no longer
turns up at the Last Signal's relay: he is in his cell.
