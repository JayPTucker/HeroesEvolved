# Invisibility, Phasing and Empathic Mimicry

Three new unique powers bring the roster to **16**. Each uses the normal one-owner rule, Eclipse
assignment, awakening intro, mastery and an 18-node skill tree. Every original tree has also
grown to 18 nodes; see the end of this page.

## Invisibility (Claude Rains)

Invisibility has been rebuilt around **Exposure**, a physical **Refraction Step**, public optical
**Mirages**, a moving **Shared Veil**, and **Ghost Protocol**. All eighteen skill nodes now support
Ghost, Trickster and Infiltrator playstyles, including false trails, prepared counterattacks,
quiet item lifting and a checked **Hidden Passage** for companions.

See the [current Invisibility guide](invisibility.md) for controls, concealment counters,
animations and the complete skill tree.

## Phasing (D.L. Hawkins)

Phasing has been reworked as **Density**: a dial from Full Ghost to Full Dense, Reach Through, Embed,
Sink and Hollow Ground. See [Density](density.md). Walking through walls at speed now belongs to
Superspeed's Vibrate.

## Empathic Mimicry (Peter Petrelli)

| Key | Level | Ability | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G | 1 | **Empathize** | Absorb the power of the player you look at within 8 blocks, or of Sylar (you get one of the powers he has stolen). Copying never takes anything from them. |
| H | 1 | **Recall** | Choose which remembered power to channel. Shift steps backwards. |
| Z | 2 | **Echo I** | Use the channeled power's **first** ability. |
| X | 3 | **Echo II** | Use its **second** ability. |
| V | 5 | **Echo III** | Use its **third** ability. |

**How echoes work:**
- Echoes run at one level below yours, with the borrowed ability's normal stamina cost and **1.5×** its cooldown.
- Ultimates (the fifth ability) can never be copied. Mimicry can't copy itself.
- You remember 1 power at level 1, 2 at level 2, 3 at levels 3–4 and 4 at level 5. Learning a new one pushes out the oldest.
- Memories survive death and relogging.

**Passive:** near another powered player you regain stamina. Overexertion scrambles which memory answers.

| Branch | Active skill | Technique, then upgrade, then capstone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Echo Hunter | Borrowed Strike | **Sense Powers** (every powered person and Sylar within 48 blocks glows), then Quick Study (Empathize at 16 blocks, no line of sight), then **Sylar's Mirror** (take everything Sylar has learned, plus one more memory slot) |
| Resonance | Empathic Surge | **Overcharge** (next Echo at full level), then Twin Echo (it recovers twice as fast), then **Peter's Burden** (Echoes cost 25% less) |
| Empathy | Calm | **Absorb Pain** (take a friend's harmful effects at half duration and heal them), then Shared Healing, then **Empath's Calm** (immune to poison and wither) |

## Every original tree is now 18 nodes

Each of the nine remaining 9-node trees gained a technique, an upgrade and a capstone in every
branch. Each branch also has a name now.

| Power | New techniques (one per branch) | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flight** | Sky Catch, Tailwind, Thunderclap | Guardian Angel cancels a teammate's fall damage; Sound Barrier buffets enemies you fly past |
| **Telekinesis** | Remote Hand, Kinetic Volley, Force Bubble | Flip levers and open doors from 24–48 blocks; Retrieve pulls items out of chests |
| **Telepathy** | **Persuasion** (level 4), Memory Walk, Hive Link | Persuasion turns a mob into your ally, gets villager discounts, or stops a player attacking you; Eden's Voice sways every hostile mob nearby; Memory Walk reads a player's power and exposes the invisible |
| **Regeneration** | Take the Wound, Adaptation, Revive | Martyr takes a teammate's killing blow; Perfect Adaptation makes you immune to fire and wither |
| **Space-Time** | Stasis Seal, Shared Blink, Accelerate Time | Freeze arrows mid-air and throw them back; blink with three friends; grow crops in seconds |
| **Radiation** | Heat Vent, Reactor Core, Glowing Path | Vent stress as a fire cone, turn stress into stamina, or poison the ground |
| **Sonic Resonance** | Shatter Note, Echo Map, Quiet Zone | Shatter glass and ice (or stone with the capstone); see ores through rock |
| **Superspeed** | Speed Mirage, **Lightning Throw**, Perception | The Flash's moves: hurl your own lightning by spending Momentum (counts as electric); **Infinite Mass Punch** at 90 Momentum; slow or stop projectiles |
| **Precognition** | Foresight, Find the Path, Shared Omen | Dodge the next 3–5 blows; find the nearest Helixite; The Painter shows the nearest village |

**Totals:** 16 powers, 288 skill nodes, 96 optional actions.
