# Invisibility, Phasing and Empathic Mimicry

Three new unique powers bring the roster to **16**. Each uses the normal one-owner rule, Eclipse
assignment, awakening intro, mastery and an 18-node skill tree. Every original tree has also
grown to 18 nodes; see the end of this page.

## Invisibility (Claude Rains)

| Key | Level | Ability | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G | 1 | **Fade** | Toggle complete invisibility. Other players can't see your body, armor, held items or name tag. Drains 2 stamina per second. Press again (or Shift) to reappear. |
| H | 1 | **Silent Step** | Dash 6 + 2 × level blocks without breaking your invisibility. |
| Z | 2 | **Unseen Strike** | Your next punch within 10 s gets +3 + level damage and blinds the target. It deals 1.5× damage if you're unseen. |
| X | 3 | **Shroud** | Make the nearest friend unseen for 15 s, or yourself if no one is near. |
| V | 5 | **Ghost Protocol** | You and friends within 6 blocks vanish for 8 s. Every mob hunting you loses track, and enemy players nearby get Darkness. |

**Passive:** mobs notice you less, and while you're unseen they have to be within 2 blocks to target you.

**Counters:**
- Taking damage flickers you visible for a second.
- Sonic's **Echo Sense**, Quake's **Tremor Sense** and Telepathy's **Memory Walk** expose you for longer.

| Branch | Active skill | Technique, then upgrade, then capstone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ambush | Blind Spot | **Smoke and Mirrors** (nearby mobs lose their target and stagger), then Lingering Mirage, then **Assassin** (Unseen Strike from invisibility deals 3×) |
| Ghost | Vanishing Guard | **Pickpocket** (lift a mob's held item), then Light Fingers, then **Phantom** (30% less damage while unseen) |
| Escape | Slip Away | **Cloak Field** (a stationary zone that hides friends), then Wider Cloak, then **Claude's Lesson** (Fade drains half as fast) |

## Phasing (D.L. Hawkins)

| Key | Level | Ability | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G | 1 | **Phase Step** | Walk straight through the wall ahead to the open space beyond (up to 2 + level blocks). Shift brings one friend. |
| H | 1 | **Intangible** | Toggle: blows, arrows, falls, blasts and falling blocks pass through you. You can't attack. Drains stamina. |
| Z | 2 | **Phase Strike** | Reach inside an enemy within 6 blocks for 6 + level damage that ignores armor. |
| X | 3 | **Phase Drop** | Sink through the floor to the next open space, up to 16 blocks. Shift rises through the ceiling instead. |
| V | 5 | **Phase Walk** | For 8 s, walking into a wall up to 3 blocks thick carries you through it. |

**Safety:**
- Every phase ends in a checked open space. You never stop inside a block.
- You can't pass bedrock, barriers, lava or protected ground (spawn protection and claims).
- You can't suffocate in walls.

| Branch | Active skill | Technique, then upgrade, then capstone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infiltrator | Through-Strike | **Phase Carry** (phase with up to 3 friends), then Thicker Walls (+4 blocks), then **Ghost Network** (intangible on arrival) |
| Density | Solidify | **Phase Lock** (sink an enemy into the ground), then Long Lock, then **Entombed** (locked enemies take damage) |
| Escape Artist | Fade Through | **Phase Fall** (Phase Drop with friends), then Deep Fall (32 blocks), then **Untouchable** (Intangible drains half as fast) |

## Empathic Mimicry (Peter Petrelli)

| Key | Level | Ability | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G | 1 | **Empathize** | Absorb the power of the player you look at within 8 blocks, or of Sylar (you get one of the powers he has stolen). Copying never takes anything from them. |
| H | 1 | **Recall** | Choose which remembered power to channel. Shift steps backwards. |
| Z | 2 | **Echo I** | Use the channeled power's **first** ability. |
| X | 3 | **Echo II** | Use its **second** ability. |
| V | 5 | **Echo III** | Use its **third** ability. |

**How echoes work:**
- Echoes run at one level below yours, with the borrowed ability's normal stamina cost and **1.5×** its cooldown.
- Ultimates (the fifth ability) can never be copied. Mimicry can't copy itself.
- You remember 1 power at level 1, 2 at level 2, 3 at levels 3–4 and 4 at level 5. Learning a new one pushes out the oldest.
- Memories survive death and relogging.

**Passive:** near another powered player you regain stamina. Overexertion scrambles which memory answers.

| Branch | Active skill | Technique, then upgrade, then capstone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Echo Hunter | Borrowed Strike | **Sense Powers** (every powered person and Sylar within 48 blocks glows), then Quick Study (Empathize at 16 blocks, no line of sight), then **Sylar's Mirror** (take everything Sylar has learned, plus one more memory slot) |
| Resonance | Empathic Surge | **Overcharge** (next Echo at full level), then Twin Echo (it recovers twice as fast), then **Peter's Burden** (Echoes cost 25% less) |
| Empathy | Calm | **Absorb Pain** (take a friend's harmful effects at half duration and heal them), then Shared Healing, then **Empath's Calm** (immune to poison and wither) |

Each of the nine remaining 9-node trees gained a technique, an upgrade and a capstone in every
branch. Each branch also has a name now.

| **Telepathy** | **Persuasion** (level 4), Memory Walk, Hive Link | Persuasion turns a mob into your ally, gets villager discounts, or stops a player attacking you; Eden's Voice sways every hostile mob nearby; Memory Walk reads a player's power and exposes the invisible |

**Totals:** 16 powers, 288 skill nodes, 96 optional actions.
